## 1. decisions.md entries

- **2026-10-08 — Complex documents: one block order (P5.18, the owner's brief).** `DocumentPage` renders its blocks in a fixed order, and a block without content is not rendered: `banner` (a cancelled document's marker, above everything) → the header (lifecycle, back, number, status, actions; counterparty, `details`, the new `currency` block, key figures) → the "Based on" `references` → the lines' card (the lines; the new `specification` summary row under them; the totals) → `notes` → `attachments` → the other `sections` → side panels as before. Phones keep the same order. New, all optional (additive): `banner`, `currency`, `references`, `specification` (ReactNode slots), `notes` and `attachments` (`DocumentBlock`: title, content, actions — drawn as SectionCards in their place), and `totals` now takes every `DocumentTotals` prop (recap, deductions, exchange, footnotes). `lines` may be null: the lines' card is then left out unless a specification or totals stand in it. `DocumentBlock` is exported.
- **2026-10-08 — DocumentReferences (P5.18, new, exported).** One line of links between the header and the lines: "Based on: Proforma PR-2026-031 · Advances A-2026-038, A-2026-044 · Contract 12/2026". Groups (`label` as shown, `kind` in the singular for the names) and items (`number`, `href`, `status`) from the application; every number a link through `linkComponent` (left to right, medium, the link colour named for every state). **How a reference shows its state in one line (P4.9 rule 7), decided:** the state stands after the number as small text (xs medium) in its tone's text colour — the words carry it, neutral is `text.secondary` — and is part of the link's accessible name ("Advance invoice A-2026-038, Paid"; the visible state is `aria-hidden` so it is not read twice). A badge per number would have made the one line a row of pills; a tooltip would hide the state from touch. Groups are separated by a decorative "·", numbers of one kind by `messages['references.listSeparator']`; the line wraps on phones; a kind without numbers is left out, and the block renders nothing when all are empty. `label` replaces the lead: "Cancels" on a cancellation document, "Corrects" on a corrective document — the back-links. Messages: `references.basedOn`, `references.lead`, `references.listSeparator`, `references.link`.
- **2026-10-08 — Read-only lines with types (P5.18, DataTable, additive).** `DataTable` takes `lineType(row)` (the types of `components/line-types.ts`, owned by D1), `lineText(row)` (default: the first column's cell) and `lineKind(row)`. A `heading` is one cell across the row in `LINE_TYPE_TEXT.heading` (bold); a `text` line one cell across the row, xs `text.secondary`; a `subtotal` puts its label end-aligned and semibold across the columns before the amounts and the amounts under them, with a 1px `border.strong` rule above (the row before it drops its own line, so there is one line, not two). **Which columns are "the amounts", decided:** the trailing run of end-aligned columns (`subtotalStart`, exported, tested) — no new column flag; with no end-aligned column the last one carries the amount. `discount` and `deduction` are lines (their negative amounts from the application). Headings, text lines and subtotals have no checkbox (`enableRowSelection` per row), no menu, and cannot be pressed. `lineKind` ("Item", "Service", "Discount") stands under the first cell as xs `text.secondary` — never a colour. **Phones** keep the types by typography: a heading or text line is a line of text in the list, a subtotal its label and the amount columns (header: value), semibold, under a `border.strong` rule (the flat list's divider replaced); a line's kind becomes its card subtitle when the card has none.
- **2026-10-08 — DocumentTotals: recap, deductions, home currency, footnotes (P5.18, additive).** Order in the block: `rows` → `recap` → `deductions` → the final row → `exchange` → `footnotes`. **Recap by tax category:** a small table under its caption (xs semibold `text.secondary`), column headers xs `text.secondary` from the application (no "VAT" in the component, D5), rows: the category as a row header, base, rate (`format.percent`), tax (`format.number` with the money decimals, or `format.money` when `recap.currency` is given); a missing rate or tax is "—", never 0. With a recap the block is **384px** (`max-w-96`) instead of 320px, so four columns of amounts fit (chosen without an owner's value). **Deductions:** one row per advance, a group with a `border.subtle` line above the first, the label a link when the row has `href` (`TotalsRow.href`, new), right before the amount due. **Foreign currency:** `exchange` (currency, home currency, rate, source, rows) after the final row: the home-currency rows as a group and the rate line in xs `text.secondary` ("1 EUR = 117,1825 RSD, NBS middle rate on 05.10.2026.", `messages['document.rate']` and `['document.rateLine']`, the rate through `format.number`, never rounded). The lines stay in the document's currency. **Footnotes:** under the totals, xs `text.secondary`, `<sup>` marker and text, given once; the lines repeat the marker in their tax column ("E¹", "AE²"). Without recap, exchange and footnotes the block is exactly P4.5's single `dl` (deductions included in it); with them it is a `role="group"` named by `label` holding the lists in order. Nothing is computed. Exported types: `TaxRecap`, `TaxRecapRow`, `TotalsExchange`, `TotalsFootnote`.
- **2026-10-08 — Currency block and notes (P5.18, new, exported).** `DocumentCurrency` (currency, home currency, rate, rate date) in DocumentPage's `currency` slot: three values in the header's style — label xs `text.secondary` above, value sm medium — "Currency EUR", "Exchange rate 1 EUR = 117,1825 RSD", "Rate date 05.10.2026." (messages `document.currency`, `document.exchangeRate`, `document.rateDate`, `document.rate`). `DocumentNotes`: template texts chosen from the application's list (`NoteTemplate`: value, label, text) and a free note (`DocumentNotesValue`: templates in order, note). View: the texts in order as paragraphs (line breaks kept); edit: a MultiSelectField of the templates by name with their texts under it (xs `text.secondary`), then a TextAreaField for the note (labels `notes.templates` "Standard texts", `notes.free` "Note", or the application's). Edit mode is the application's (the examples use an "Edit notes" / "Done" pair in the block's header; in a record-style whole-page edit the Core passes `mode="edit"` with the page's). `chosenNoteTexts` and `hasNotes` (exported, tested): a template no longer in the list is left out; a document without notes passes no `notes` block.
- **2026-10-08 — Long specification: a summary row and a full-screen sheet (P5.18, new `DocumentSpecification`).** In the lines' card, under the lines, one row: "Specification of works: 300 positions, 537.836,68 RSD" (`messages['specification.summary'](title, count, countText, amountText)`, the count and amount through `format`) with "Open" (neutral, secondary, Maximize2; `specification.open`). **Full page or sheet, decided per D14: a full-screen sheet** (the Sheet primitive's `full` side): it is a read-only view of a part of the document, so the document stays under it, Escape and the close button return to it with the focus on "Open", and it needs no route of its own — group D1's `/projects/IS-2026-007/specification` is the editing page (EditableGrid), and a second page at a neighbouring address for the same data would have been two patterns. The sheet: title and an optional description in a header with a line under it, the body scrolling; the application puts a DataTable with `lineType` (groups as headings, their subtotals) and `stickyHeader` in it, then DocumentTotals with the same recap. Controlled (`open`, `onOpenChange`) or not.
  - **300 positions: virtualised (measured, decided).** 324 rows (12 groups × 25 positions + headings + subtotals), 9 columns. Measured on the built Storybook ("Examples / Interim situation with a specification"), Chromium of Playwright 1.63 on the owner's Windows machine while other test runs shared it, from the press on "Open" (or a 2,000px scroll jump) to the next painted frame, five times each; "4×" is Chrome's CPU throttling. **Without virtualisation** (every row drawn, full text): opening 820–987ms, 4× 6.7–10.6s — too slow. **With DataTable `virtualize`** (44px rows, one line each, 8 rows of overscan; the full size announced with `aria-rowcount`): opening 50–109ms, 4× 0.9–1.7s; scrolling 21–50ms, 4× 345–590ms (as P3.2's 1,000 cards). The position texts are short enough for one line; a longer text ends with "…" and is whole in the row's accessible content. The examples and the story use `virtualize` with `maxHeight="62vh"`.
- **2026-10-08 — Corrective documents are DocumentPages (P5.18, decided: no template of its own).** A decrease or increase document is a DocumentPage with "Corrects: Invoice F-2026-0410" references (several invoices for a period: one group with several numbers, and a "Document" column in the lines), its lines as **Original / Change / New** columns and the totals of the change. New: `correctionColumns(spec)` (exported) makes the three DataTable columns of one corrected value — `<id>.original`, `<id>.change`, `<id>.new` — headers from `messages['correction.original' | 'correction.change' | 'correction.new']` or the application's (one call for the quantity, one for the amount); `ChangeText` (exported) writes a change with its sign, medium weight, never rounded, "—" when empty. **New in `format` (additive):** `number` and `money` take `sign: 'always'` — "+" before a value above zero (zero, negative values and text unchanged; tested); `withSign` is exported from `provider/format.ts`. A template would only have renamed DocumentPage's slots.
- **2026-10-08 — Cancellation: one dialog, then a marker on the page (P5.18).** **IrreversibleConfirmDialog gains `reason`** (additive): with it, the same dialog asks why — a required text, or a choice from `reason.reasons` with optional details, exactly as ReasonConfirmDialog — and for the typed number; the confirm button enables once both are given, and `onConfirm(answer)` receives `{ reason, text }` (without `reason` the answer is empty and callers that ignore it work unchanged). The reason comes first, the typed number last (the thinking, then the decision); Cancel keeps the focus (P4.9d). One pattern, never two dialogs in a row. After it the application shows the status "Cancelled" (neutral badge) and **`CancellationBanner`** (new, exported) in DocumentPage's `banner` slot: the Banner look, tone **neutral by default** (a cancelled document is a state, not an error; `tone="danger"` for a louder Core), the Ban icon, "Cancelled" (`document.cancelledTitle`) then "Cancelled by Milica Petrović on 06.10.2026. at 11:20. Reason: …" (`document.cancelledBy(name, dateText, timeText)` with `format.date` and `format.time` — the tenant's time — and `document.cancelReason`) and the link to the cancellation document ("Cancellation document ST-2026-0004"). The cancellation document links back with `DocumentReferences label="Cancels"`. **The PDF's marker is the Core's** (the print template draws "CANCELLED" with the cancellation's number; the Design System draws only the screen).
- **When to use which (for the integrator's one rule):** LifecycleBar — the dots above a document, where it stands in its own life; DocumentReferences — the documents it is based on or corrects/cancels, one line under the header; RelatedDocuments (side panel) — every related document with its badge; CancellationBanner — a cancelled document's marker; StatusTimeline (group B) — the states of one process with the next step; HistoryList (group A) — the full history.
