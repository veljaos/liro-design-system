## Decision entries (for `docs/decisions.md`)

- **2026-10-14 — PageViewer, not DocumentFrame, beside recognised fields (P5.31).** A recognised
  value must show where it was read: a region on the page, given by the application in the page's
  own units. DocumentFrame shows a viewer that runs on another origin in a sandbox; the host can
  neither see its drawing nor mark on it, and pointing at a region would need a version 2 of the
  frame protocol and every viewer to implement it. The recognition service already has the pages
  as images (it read them), so the library draws them itself: **PageViewer** (`page-viewer.tsx`,
  logic in `page-viewer-logic.ts`, tested) shows a document's pages as the application's images —
  a scan, a photo, a rendered PDF page — one at a time, with DocumentFrame's toolbar (previous /
  next page, "Page 1 of 2", zoom 50–300% in DocumentFrame's steps; 100% fits the width, more
  scrolls both ways) and one region highlighted (`regions`, `highlight`). The page is paper: light
  in both themes (`data-liro-theme="light"`, as PrintPage) and never mirrored in right-to-left
  (`dir="ltr"`, as FloorPlan's canvas); regions stand where they were measured, from the page's
  top-left corner, as percentages of the page (`regionBox`). A new highlight turns to its page
  (reported with `onPageChange`) and is scrolled into view; the first one is not scrolled to, so a
  picture never moves. "Highlighted: Total, page 1 of 2" is announced politely. States: loading (a
  page-shaped skeleton), `error` with "Try again", and `unavailable` — the application's own words
  and actions in place of the pages (a PDF locked with a password). DocumentFrame stays the viewer
  for a document the application shows from another origin (a signing service's preview).
- **2026-10-14 — The highlight is a marker pen (P5.31).** The region is status.warning.bg
  multiplied over the page (`mix-blend-multiply`: the printed text stays readable through it, as
  the search's matches on warning.bg) inside a 2px status.warning.fg frame, its name on a raised
  tag above it (below it in the page's top tenth, `labelSide`). Not warning.solid: the solid is
  allowed only where `tone-usage.test.ts` lists it, and the paper is light in both themes anyway.
  Not blue (D17): a highlight is not a selection or an action.
- **2026-10-14 — RecognizedField: the application's confidence, never the library's verdict
  (P5.31).** It wraps any field of the library; `children(describedBy)` renders the field with the
  note's id, so the confidence is read with the field. Under the field one xs line: "Confidence:
  98%" in the application's words (`confidence` is a string: "98%", "from SEF", "not on the
  document"); a reading the application calls uncertain (`low`) has a TriangleAlert and "Low
  confidence" (or the application's `lowLabel`, "Not found on the page") in status.warning.fg,
  weight 500 — never colour alone. The component never turns a figure into "low" (the D30 rule for
  HealthList). Once the user changes the value, "Changed by you" with a Pencil replaces it. "Show
  where it was read" (`onShowSource`) is a link-styled button (text.link, ScanSearch, 24px high);
  while its region is shown (`sourceShown`) it is pressed (`aria-pressed`) with the neutral
  selection. ChangeableValue stays for a value the system filled in from its own records.
- **2026-10-14 — The document inbox is built from the library (P5.31).** The inbox is ListPage +
  FilterBar + DataTable: type, sender, received, source, amount and the state in words and tone
  (New, Recognised, Needs review, Posted, Rejected, Duplicate — the six of the plan; the
  application's map `DOC_TONES`); a new document's condition stands in words under its state
  (Recognising…, Not recognised: enter by hand, Locked with a password, Scan cannot be read), so
  the six states stay six. Views To review, Posted, Rejected and duplicates, All. "Add documents"
  is a FileDropzone above the card ("Choose files" is the way, dropping the shortcut; "Take a
  photo" on phones, `camera`), its description names the inbox's e-mail address; the main action
  "Review 11 documents" beside it. Checked rows: "Review" and "Reject…" (ReasonConfirmDialog, the
  count in the question). A done document opens in a QuickPreview with its page.
- **2026-10-14 — The review is decided document by document (P5.31).** WorklistPage, as the bank
  statement's lines (P5.23): the documents to review at the side (the queue is fixed when the
  review opens; a decided document stays in it with its new state), one in focus: its page
  (PageViewer) beside the recognised values (a container query: side by side from 48rem of the
  detail's width, the page first below it), then the lines (EditableGrid: description, unit,
  quantity, price, amount, account; the suggested account's reason as a line detail, an uncertain
  reading as a warning message under its row). The supplier is a LookupField: matched in the
  catalog by the tax number read ("Matched in your suppliers by tax number 106543218."), or "Not
  in your suppliers: tax number 109876543 was read" with "+ Create supplier". VAT by rate and the
  total as MoneyFields, a rate the document does not have empty ("not on the document").
  Decisions in the detail's bar, the main one last: Save for later, Mark as duplicate…
  (CandidateList of the application's candidates for the original, one choice), Reject… (the
  application's reasons), Confirm and post ("Confirm and import" for a bank statement, "Confirm
  and file" for a contract or other); a toast with Undo, and the next document still to review
  opens. **Keyboard first:** Tab goes through the fields; Enter in a field confirms once the field
  has read its text (the decision waits one task), never inside an open list or the lines grid. A
  photo of a document already in the inbox shows DuplicateWarning with the original's link.
- **2026-10-14 — A document that cannot be read yet (P5.31).** Recognising: the page and skeleton
  fields with "Recognising the document…"; Confirm is unavailable with "Recognition is still
  running.". Recognition failed: a warning Alert and plain fields to enter by hand (no confidence
  — nothing was read). A PDF locked with a password: PageViewer's `unavailable` with "Enter the
  password…" (a Dialog with PasswordField; the password is used once and not kept), Confirm
  unavailable with "The file is locked with a password.". An unreadable scan: the blurred page, a
  warning Alert with "Upload a better copy" and "Enter by hand" (neutral secondary, D23).
- **2026-10-14 — ValueComparison (P5.33).** The same things as two sources record them, side by
  side — our open items beside a partner's statement, a payment as each side booked it. A table:
  the row's name, the two sources' values (figures tabular at the end), the result in words —
  "Same" with a Check (text.secondary), or "Differs" or the application's `note` ("Only in their
  statement", "Dates differ") with a TriangleAlert in status.warning.fg on a status.warning.bg row;
  "2 of 4 differ" above it, announced politely. Phones: one card per row, each value labelled with
  its source. It compares nothing: which rows differ, and why, is the application's (`differs`,
  `note`). Loading: skeleton rows; no rows: `empty` ("Nothing to compare"). Helper
  `valueComparisonCounts` (the name `comparisonCounts` is LegalCompare's).
- **2026-10-14 — IOS: the statements of open items are built from the library (P5.33).** The
  partners are ListPage + FilterBar + DataTable: balance at the cut-off (the sum of the open items,
  whole paras), the statement's number, date, way and opening, the state (Not sent, Sent, Opened,
  Confirmed, Disputed, No answer; the application's map: on its way blue, confirmed green, disputed
  warning — it waits on us —, no answer after the deadline danger, not sent neutral) with the answer
  date, the reminder or their balance under it. "Prepare statements…" takes the checked partners
  (or every one not sent): one Dialog with the cut-off date, "Send as" (a signed PDF through Liro
  Bridge where the partner has it — the data says, "2 of 4 partners have Liro Bridge; the others
  get an e-mail." — or e-mail), the statements to send, and Preview… (the statement as the partner
  receives it, PrintDocument: from / to, the open items, the balance, what to do by when; no
  signature field, it is signed electronically). "Send reminder…" goes only to partners that have
  the statement and have not answered; the others are named in the question.
- **2026-10-14 — The partner's side is Liro's partner portal (P5.33).** PortalShell ("Liro ·
  Partners"): Jelena Popović of Elektro Sistem d.o.o. sees Kvadrat Gradnja d.o.o.'s statement —
  its details, the open items, "Kvadrat Gradnja d.o.o. owes you 180.000,00 RSD" — and Signed PDF,
  Dispute…, Confirm (main last). Dispute is an end Drawer (a short edit, D14): the balance in their
  books (required), what differs (required), their own list of open items (FileDropzone, optional);
  Confirm is a ConfirmDialog titled by the action with the statement as subject (D22). The portal's
  stories show the statement as she saw it before answering; the dataset has her dispute at 08:55.
- **2026-10-14 — A disputed statement is reconciled line by line (P5.33).** WorklistPage on the
  bank statement's pattern: the statement's documents at the side (Matches, To resolve, Resolved),
  one in focus with our books beside their statement (ValueComparison), the differences marked;
  "How is this difference resolved?" with the application's ways, its suggestion preselected and
  said; a note to the partner; "Resolve and next" with Undo. Above: our balance, theirs, the
  difference, what is not explained yet (warning while it is not 0) and "0 of 2 differences
  resolved". "All items side by side" shows every document in one ValueComparison; "Send our
  answer" is unavailable until nothing is left ("2 differences are not resolved yet."), then a
  ConfirmDialog with each resolution. Elektro Sistem's difference of 120.000,00 RSD is two items:
  ES-2026-0533 (36.000,00, 28.09.2026), theirs only — it reached our document inbox through SEF on
  13.10.2026, linked from the line —, and our payment of ES-2026-0498 (84.000,00) of 30.09.2026
  (bank statement 187), booked by them on 01.10.2026.
