## Decision entries (for `docs/decisions.md`)

- **2026-10-14 — HierarchyChart is drawn in HTML, not on a canvas (P5.42, decided by the lead).**
  An organisation chart, units, a group of companies and cost centres are one generic component,
  `HierarchyChart`: nested boxes with the connector lines drawn by CSS borders (border.strong),
  tokens only. Not React Flow or dagre (the diagram subpath's libraries): in HTML right-to-left
  mirrors by itself (the levels are flex rows, the lines logical: the half bars at the start and
  end of each child, the drop at its centre), the pictures are stable (no measured layout, no
  canvas), print works on a landscape PrintPage, text wraps inside its 224px box and can be
  selected, and assistive technology reads real elements. No new dependency.
- **2026-10-14 — The chart's layout (P5.42).** Top-down; the first `defaultExpandedDepth` levels
  (default 3) are open. A node with children has a 24px round toggle on its line: "+N" (how many
  stand under it, every level) to open, a minus to close; it is a pointer's way — the keyboard opens
  and closes with Space on the node, so the toggle is not a tab stop. Children that are all leaves
  (from `stackFrom`, default 3: a crew, a team) stand as one bordered list under their parent, so a
  wide level never grows the chart sideways without end. A box: the avatar when the node is a person
  (PersonAvatar), the title sm semibold, the subtitle xs text.secondary, the detail xs
  text.tertiary, a StatusBadge, the application's count in words ("39 people under", "14 people").
  A vacant position: a dashed border and its "Vacant" mark (words, never only the dashes). The words
  on the line to the parent (an ownership share) stand on the line in a small raised tag. The chart
  scrolls in both directions inside its card (`maxHeight`, default 70vh); the page never scrolls
  sideways (D19). At first, and whenever another unit is shown, the top node (or the search's match)
  is brought to the middle of the area — inside it only, the page never moves. "Fit to width" scales
  the chart to the card (CSS `zoom`, so the scroll area stays right), "Actual size" back: two
  pressed buttons, no dragging (WCAG 2.5.7).
- **2026-10-14 — Choosing, a unit alone, the search (P5.42).** A press or Enter on a node chooses it
  — the neutral selection (surface.selected, a border.selected border; D17) — and calls
  `onOpen(id)`: the application opens its card (a Drawer) or page. "Show only this unit" draws the
  chosen node's subtree alone; above it the path back up as a trail of buttons ("›" between them,
  not Breadcrumbs: those are links of the application's router, these change the view) and "Show
  the whole chart". The search filters the titles, subtitles and details here (`matchHierarchy`, in
  the provider's locale), or asks the application (`onSearch`, its `matches`); the first match is
  revealed (its path opened, the whole chart shown if it lies outside the unit), chosen and brought
  into view; Enter in the field goes to the next; "1 of 3 found" is announced politely.
  `hierarchyPath` (pure, tested) gives the path to a node.
- **2026-10-14 — Secondary relations are listed, never drawn (P5.42).** A dotted-line relation
  (Milica Petrović works with the external accountant; the planning engineer also reports to Sales)
  is the application's data, listed in the node's card; lines across the chart would cross the
  reporting lines and cannot be read by assistive technology.
- **2026-10-14 — The chart is a WAI-ARIA tree (P5.42).** `role="tree"`, every node a `treeitem`
  (named by its title, subtitle and the vacant mark) with aria-level, aria-setsize, aria-posinset,
  aria-expanded and aria-selected, children in a `group`; one node in the tab order (roving focus).
  Keys by the chart's direction (`hierarchyKeyAction`, tested): ArrowDown to the first child
  (opening the node), ArrowUp to the parent, ArrowLeft / ArrowRight to the siblings — the arrow
  toward the reading direction's end is "next", so they are mirrored in right-to-left —, along a
  stacked list with ArrowDown / ArrowUp, Home and End, Enter opens the card, Space opens or closes,
  `*` opens the siblings. Phones (below 48em, or `layout`): the same nodes as TreeView's indented
  list (single selection = choosing, which calls `onOpen`), with the same search and unit; the count,
  badge and line words at the row's end.
- **2026-10-14 — The chart on paper (P5.42).** `toolbar={false}` leaves the search, unit and size
  controls and the toggles out, `framed={false}` the card (D27: no frames on paper); on a landscape
  PrintPage with every level open, stacked lists and "Fit to width" the chart fits the sheet.
