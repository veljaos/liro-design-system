## 1. Decision entries (for `docs/decisions.md`)

- **2026-10-13 — Every one-record dialog in the working-time and warehouse components names the
  action, then the record (P5.28 item 6).** The remaining dialogs that acted on one record with
  the record inside a sentence-title now use the shared `DialogHeading` / `subject`:
  AttendanceGrid's correction ("Correct" / "Ivan Stojanović, Tue 29"), ShiftPlanner's "Move to…"
  dialog ("Move" / "Early · Teodora Kovačević, Sunday, October 11, 2026": the shift, whose it is
  and its day — the template's name alone does not say which record), PickList's skip ("Skip
  line" / the item), and ResourceSchedule's "Move to…" popover ("Move to…" / the booking, bold
  on its own line under it; the popover keeps its name "Move to…: Marko Ilić"). Messages
  `attendance.correctTitle`, `shifts.moveTitle` and `pick.skipTitle` are removed; new
  `shifts.moveSubject`. Searched for the rest of the class in AttendanceGrid, ShiftPlanner,
  MyShifts, ResourceSchedule, CalendarView, Timetable, DataTable, EditableGrid, RoomRack,
  PickList, StockCount, CoursePage, TreeView, KanbanBoard, ImportWizard, BulkEditDrawer,
  LookupCreateDrawer, DuplicateWarning, JobProgress and their stories and examples: none else
  acts on one record without its subject (KanbanBoard's "Move to" is a menu named by the card;
  BulkEditDrawer, ImportWizard, BulkActionBar and the rotation dialog act on several or none and
  keep a question or a count; LookupCreateDrawer and DuplicateWarning's reason create a record
  that does not exist yet; record views — a room, a bed, a bin — are titled by the record).
- **2026-10-13 — A move in ResourceSchedule only proposes (P5.28 item 9).** The schedule reports
  `onMove` and draws the booking where the application has it; a drag, the keys or "Move to…"
  never move it by themselves. Where the booking is something others rely on (an appointment, a
  reservation, a stay) the application confirms it with ChangeConfirmDialog before changing
  `bookings` or messaging anyone. The component's own stories now do the same (their harness
  asks "Move booking" / the booking with doctor or room and time old → new and an unchecked
  "Notify …"; Save moves it; the readout says "message sent" or "no message"); new story "Move
  asked". The clinic example already did; the hotel's bookings do not yet (below, for the lead).
- **2026-10-13 — Messages on publishing and other confirmations are a choice (P5.28 item 9).**
  `ConfirmDialog` (and `DeleteConfirmDialog`) gained `notify?: readonly NotifyOption[]` and
  `notifyConfirmLabel`: the messages the application can send with the action stand as "Notify
  …" checkboxes under the text (the same `NotifyChoices` group as ChangeConfirmDialog's, "Messages
  to send"), each checked as the application sets it and reset each time the dialog opens; while
  one is checked the button says `notifyConfirmLabel`; `onConfirm` receives `{ notify }` with the
  checked ids (empty without `notify`). A callback without a parameter still fits, so existing
  callers are unchanged. ReasonConfirmDialog and IrreversibleConfirmDialog do not take it.
  ShiftPlanner: `publishNotify` (the application's messages) and `onPublish({ notify })`
  (backward compatible: a callback without a parameter still fits; without `publishNotify`
  publishing messages nobody and `notify` is empty); the button says "Publish and notify" /
  "Publish changes and notify" while a message is checked (`shifts.publishAndNotify`,
  `shifts.publishChangesAndNotify`); `defaultPublishOpen` pictures the confirmation. The store
  offers "Notify 8 people in the app" (checked) and "Send an SMS to the 2 people whose shifts
  changed" (not checked); the emergency department "Notify 12 people in the app" (checked); the
  toasts say what went ("Changes published. 8 people get a message in the app. 2 also get an
  SMS." / "… see their shifts in the app; no message was sent."). Decided by me: one ConfirmDialog
  prop rather than a second dialog, because publishing is a confirmation of an action, not a
  change old → new.
