# Lead's notes: merge values, sticky first columns, IPS QR and payment orders (P5.31–P5.40)

## Decisions

- **2026-10-14 — Every sticky first column on a layer of its own (P5.31).** DataTable (in a card,
  with or without the selection column), AttendanceGrid, ShiftPlanner, ResourceSchedule and
  EditableGrid now use `STICKY_START` (and `STICKY_START_AFTER_SELECT` after DataTable's 48px
  selection column), as StatutoryFormPage has since P5.29. One compositing layer, one
  antialiasing: the same story gives one picture. Announced in P5.29's report; done here because
  every picture is made again anyway.
- **2026-10-14 — Merge values: saved replies and previews filled in (the owner's request, P5.31).**
  A merge field takes the application's value: plain text, or a typed value (date, instant, time,
  number, amount as decimal strings) written through the provider's `format` — never rounded
  (D4). A field may be **computed**: `computed: 'today'` is the provider's `today` (moved by whole
  days with `offsetDays`), `computed: 'now'` the application's `now` (never the device's clock,
  D25); the key is the application's ("date_now"). A field without a value stays a pill marked
  "No value" (dashed, warning tone, its name read) and a line under the text names it; nothing is
  replaced by an empty string or a guess. RichText (`mergeValues`) previews a template with sample
  values; RichTextField and ReplyComposer (`mergeValues`, `now`) write the values in for the first
  text and for everything inserted (a saved reply, a field chosen from "{{"); a template editor
  passes no values and keeps pills. `resolveMergeText` fills a plain-text template (a subject, an
  SMS). The library writes values; it never computes business values.
- **2026-10-14 — QR codes are real (P5.40).** QrCode encodes any text in the browser (`qr` 0.7.2,
  MIT OR Apache-2.0, used under MIT, no dependencies; published 2026-09-28) and draws one SVG
  path, dark on the light surface in both themes (`data-liro-theme="light"`, as paper), with a
  4-module quiet zone, medium error correction, named by `label`; `size` in px or `fill` (a
  printout's slot). A text too long for any code shows the provider's message, never a broken
  code. **Fixed as a class:** every illustrative, unreadable code in stories and examples
  (StoryCode, `illustrativeCode`, a lucide icon standing in for a fiscal code) is now a real
  QrCode; `story-code.tsx` and `illustrativeCode` are removed.
- **2026-10-14 — IPS NBS QR text (P5.40).** `ipsQrText(payment)` writes the IPS QR text of the
  National Bank of Serbia from the application's payment and names every problem by field
  (`IpsProblem`, words from `messages['ips.problem']`): tags K|V|C|R|N|I|P|SF|S|M|JS|RO|RL|RP in
  that order; K PR (a bill), PT (shown by the merchant), PK (shown by the buyer), EK; the payee's
  account normalised to 18 digits and its MOD 97 control number checked; the amount "RSD294000,50"
  from the decimal string (decimal comma, no separators, 0,01 to 14 whole digits; more than two
  decimals is a problem, never rounded, D4); SF required on a bill (3 digits, first 1 or 2); M
  required at a point of sale; RO the model and number (model 97's control number checked, written
  without dashes; no model is "00"); lengths 70 / 35 / 140; Latin letters (Serbian ones too),
  digits and common punctuation, never "|". A national payment standard is a format, like a
  locale's digits; what is paid is the application's. **Source:** ips.nbs.rs was blocked by the
  environment's network policy, so the guidelines PDF was not read; the rules come from two public
  MIT implementations (`ips-qr-code`, `nbs-ips-qr`, read only, not installed) and are checked
  against a known valid code. To confirm with the NBS validator when the site is reachable.
- **2026-10-14 — Payment orders as their real forms (P5.40).** PaymentOrderForm draws a bank's
  paper payment order — in Serbia forms 1 (nalog za uplatu), 2 (nalog za isplatu) and 3 (nalog za
  prenos) — with its boxes (an official form is its real grid, D28), black on white in both
  themes; every word is the application's (the official Serbian labels in the examples). The IPS
  QR code stands on forms 1 and 3; a withdrawal pays out cash and has none. The examples' screen
  (Banking → Payment orders) checks the order when the user prints or downloads, each problem
  under its field and the first focused.
- **2026-10-14 — The till's two instant payments (P5.40).** The till shows the merchant's IPS QR
  code (PT: the merchant category and the sale's 19-digit reference), or the cashier scans the
  buyer's code (PK) with ScanField; the bank's answer is the application's (`cardState`).
  PaymentDialog draws no frame for an empty code.
- **2026-10-14 — Valid control numbers in the examples (P5.40).** The examples' account numbers
  and model 97 references now carry valid control numbers (160-0000012345678-67,
  160-0000000456789-28, "97 32-2026-0412", "97 14-2026-0418", "97 38-2026-0410"), so every IPS
  code scans as a bank would accept it.
- **2026-10-14 — One set of plan names (P5.36).** Liro's plans are Start, Business and Pro, also
  in the Superadmin's tenants; the administration's seats are a UsageMeter.
