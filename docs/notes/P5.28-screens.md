## 1. Decision entries (for `docs/decisions.md`)

- **2026-10-13 — A dialog on one record: the action, then the record bold (P5.28 item 6, the
  owner: "'Correct' / 'Nenad Ilić, sre 7'").** A dialog, drawer or confirmation that acts on ONE
  record names the action as its title ("Correct", "Move appointment", "Reject swap request",
  "Show diagnosis", "Post statement") and the record, bold (700, text.primary), on its own line
  under it ("Nenad Ilić, Wed 7", "UF-2026-1187 · Medic Lab Niš d.o.o."). One shared way:
  `subject` on `Dialog`, `Drawer`, `ConfirmDialog`, `DeleteConfirmDialog`,

  draw their own header from the primitives put `DialogHeading` (components/dialog.tsx, not
  exported from the package) inside their `DialogTitle` and give `DialogHeader` the class
  `dialogHeaderClass(subject)` ('items-start': the close button stays on the title's line). The
  record is **inside the title element**, after a space, so the dialog's accessible name says
  both ("Correct Nenad Ilić, Wed 7"). A confirmation about several records or none keeps a
  question as its title ("Approve 12 invoices?", "Leave without saving?"). Applied:
  ClockRecordList's correction ("Correct clock record" / person, date), ShiftSwapDialog (the
  shift is the subject; its sunken box is gone, its place is the description), MaskedValue (the

  DocumentPage stories, and the examples (swap rejection and approval, bank statement posting,

  `clock.correctSubject`; `diagram.connectTitle` is a string ("Connect to"); `diagram.renameTitle`

  new `attendance.correctAction`, `attendance.correctSubject`, `shifts.moveAction`,
  `pick.skipAction` for the components other helpers own (below, "For the lead").

  "Excuse today's absences" inside the yellow warning).** An action inside an Alert, a Banner, a

  patient gets a message.'").** `ChangeConfirmDialog` (components/change-confirm-dialog.tsx): a

  a bed only PROPOSES the change; the application opens this dialog and applies it on Save.
