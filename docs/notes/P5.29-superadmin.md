# P5.29 b — Superadmin (helper "superadmin", examples R)

Incidents, security events, tenants with an audited "view as", and announcements, for the Liro
team working across tenants. System status and Network are another helper's.

## Decision entries (for `docs/decisions.md`)

- **2026-10-14 — The Superadmin is built from the templates; nothing new was needed (P5.29 b).**
  Incidents: ListPage (views All / Open / Monitoring / Resolved, FilterBar with severity and
  service, DataTable) and an incident page on DocumentPage. Security events: WorklistPage.
  Tenants: ListPage with a row menu ("View as admin…", "Send an announcement…"). The tenant's
  view: AppShell's `impersonationBar` slot with ImpersonationBar over a read-only DetailPage.
  Announcements: ListPage and an editor on RecordFormPage. Every figure, state, duration, count
  and text is the application's (examples' `data-R.ts`); the screens filter, count and change it.
  The only library change is one optional prop (ImpersonationBar `note`, below).
- **2026-10-14 — An incident on DocumentPage (P5.29 b).** The h1 is the number and the title
  ("INC-2026-042 · SEF submissions delayed"); the lifecycle bar is the incident's stages
  (Detected, Investigating, Identified, Monitoring, Resolved); the key figures are severity,
  start, duration ("Going on for" while open, "Lasted" after) and the tenants affected. No lines
  (`lines={null}`): the sections in order are the status-page message (an Alert in the tone tenants
  see, with who posted it and when), the timeline (StatusTimeline: every update a step with its
  text and author, "· on the status page" when public; an incident not yet acknowledged shows
  "Not acknowledged — Paged … at 08:52 and again at 09:07" as the warning next step), the affected
  tenants (a KeyValueList, tenant → the application's impact) and the postmortem (RichText; before
  resolution "Written after the incident is resolved.", after it "Due by …" until written). Side
  panels: People (assignee, incident commander, on call, second on call), Affected services,
  Related incidents. Actions, the main last: Resolve… (positive, secondary), Post update…, and
  Acknowledge (primary) while nobody has; once acknowledged, Post update… is the primary.
  Post update is a Dialog with the incident as `subject`: the stage (radios), the message, and
  "Also post it to the status page" as an explicit checkbox. Resolve is a ConfirmDialog with the
  subject and two `notify` choices (post "Resolved" to the status page, checked; e-mail the
  affected tenants' administrators, not checked) — "Resolve and notify" (D24). **Phones see and
  acknowledge:** no header actions on a phone; Acknowledge stands in the bottom bar while the
  incident is not acknowledged.
- **2026-10-14 — Security events are a WorklistPage (P5.29 b).** Each event is decided one at a
  time — start investigating, then close it with the action taken — with its facts beside the
  queue, Previous / Next and "3 of 7"; a ListPage would open each event on a page of its own and
  lose the queue. The queue opens on what is not closed (State: New, Investigating), the most
  severe first, then the newest (the application's order). The row: kind, tenant · time,
  severity as the figure, state as the status. The summary above: New, Investigating, "High, not
  closed" (danger) and Closed. The 380px list holds the FilterBar with every filter in its drawer
  (`inline={0}`), the chosen ones as pills. Closing asks the action taken from the Core's list
  (ReasonConfirmDialog, details optional) and records it in the event and its history; checked
  rows close together with the same question and the count.
- **2026-10-14 — "View as" a tenant: the reason first, then an audited, read-only bar (P5.29 b).**
  A tenant's row (click, Enter, or the row menu) asks why first (ReasonConfirmDialog: "Support
  ticket", "Incident", "The tenant's own request", with the ticket or incident number as
  details; its message says the view is read-only and recorded). The tenant's administration then
  opens in the tenant's own shell (its brand, its company) under ImpersonationBar: "Viewing as
  Kvadrat Gradnja d.o.o. administrator · Read-only · Reason: … · Every page you open is recorded
  in the audit log", "Exit view" at the end (also in the user menu). The page itself is read-only
  values and tables, no actions. ImpersonationBar gained an optional `note` (the application's
  text after the reason) rather than a second bar or a longer `mode`: the mode stays one word
  ("Read-only"), and the audit fact reads as its own part of the strip.
- **2026-10-14 — Announcements: the editor shows the Banner as tenants will see it (P5.29 b).**
  RecordFormPage with four FormSections: Message (kind: "Banner in the application" or
  "Status-page message", tone, title, message), Preview (the Banner for a banner, the Alert for a
  status-page message, live as the user types; a sentence while both texts are empty), Audience
  (all tenants, or chosen tenants through a MultiSelectField, searchable by name with the tax
  number as the option's description), Schedule (start and end dates with DateField, times from a
  half-hour SelectField — 48 options, so searched by typing, D20; the library has no time field
  and needs none for this). The checks are the application's: a title for a draft; the message,
  at least one chosen tenant, a start, and an end after the start to schedule. Actions by state:
  a draft or a new one — Cancel, Save draft, Schedule (primary); scheduled — Cancel, Move to
  drafts, Save changes; live — Cancel, End now… (caution, a ConfirmDialog with the subject), Save
  changes; ended — read-only fields and "Duplicate as new". The list: views All / Draft / Scheduled
  / Live / Ended, Kind and Tone filters, a draft's dates "—", a live one without an end "When
  ended". A tenant's row menu "Send an announcement…" opens a new one with that tenant chosen.
- **2026-10-14 — The Superadmin's statuses in the examples' one tone map (P5.29 b).** Incidents:
  Open danger, Monitoring warning, Resolved success. Security events: New warning (waits on
  someone), Investigating info (on its way), Closed neutral. Severity: SEV-1 / High danger, SEV-2 /
  Medium warning, SEV-3 / Low neutral. Tenants' health: Healthy success, Degraded warning,
  Failing danger, Suspended danger (existing). Announcements: Scheduled info, Live success, Ended
  neutral, Draft neutral (existing).
- **2026-10-14 — Ticket numbers in example text are written without "#" (P5.29 b).**
  `liro/no-raw-colors` reads "#4821" as a colour literal (four hex digits, `#RGBA`), so the
  examples write "Support ticket 4821". The helpdesk's examples meet the same rule; see "For the
  lead".
