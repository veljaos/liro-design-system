## Decisions (for `docs/decisions.md`)

- **2026-10-16 — University from what exists (P5.73).** The Illustrative Faculty of Engineering,
  Novi Sad (labelled illustrative) is assembled from the library; nothing new was needed. The
  student portal is PortalShell (made for a phone first; sections My exams, Registration with the
  note "Open", Grades). Exam periods and the examiner's records are DataTables in the AppShell
  (cards on phones); the periods' state is in words (Upcoming, Registration open, Grading,
  Finished; never blue: Registration open success, Grading warning, the rest neutral).
- **2026-10-16 — Exam registration as a CandidateList (P5.73).** The exams open to the student
  are a CandidateList with checkboxes (several at once): the subtitle is year, ECTS, date and room;
  the reason carries the application's conditions, which registration it is and the fee's rule;
  the figure is the fee ("Free" or the amount). Under it the total to pay (the application's sum
  of the chosen fees) and Register (UnavailableAction "Choose at least one exam." while nothing
  is chosen); one ConfirmDialog whose title is a question about several records (D22: "Register
  for 2 exams?"). Exams not open to her are a plain list with the reason in words after a Ban
  icon ("Not allowed: …"), never a disabled checkbox. Registered exams are a DataTable with
  "Withdraw registration" in the row menu (ConfirmDialog, caution, the course as `subject`). The
  deadline is a neutral Alert. Registrations are kept above the screens (`useExampleState`), so
  My exams shows what was registered.
- **2026-10-16 — The exam record (zapisnik) as a DocumentPage with an EditableGrid (P5.73).** One
  row per registered student (fixed rows: `minRows` = `maxRows`): index, student, attempt and
  pre-exam points read-only; the exam's points (`number`) and the grade (`select` of the
  application's scale plus "Did not take the exam") entered from the keyboard; the total and the
  ECTS earned are the application's display columns. The application's checks are GridMessages
  under the row (more points than the exam has: danger; a grade the scale does not give for the
  total, points for an absent student: warning). Key figures: registered, graded "12 of 14"
  (warning until complete), passed, the average of those who passed. Side panels: Signatures
  (SignerList: examiner, then head of department) and the grading scale (KeyValueList). The
  examiner may sign only when every student has a grade (the application's rule): until then the
  examiner is not `current` and a sentence says how many are missing. Sign opens a ConfirmDialog
  (the record as `subject`); signed, every column is read-only, the status badge "Locked" (lock
  icon), the lifecycle "Signed by the examiner" and a neutral Alert banner says who signed when
  and that corrections go through student services.
- **2026-10-16 — Grades and averages are the application's (P5.73).** The scale (grade, words,
  ECTS letter, lowest points), the totals of points, the grade a total gives, the average (two
  decimals, every passed exam once) and the ECTS sum are played in `data-BN.ts` with exact
  decimal arithmetic (`toUnits`/`paras`, `compareDecimal`, data-H's `averageOf`) and checked when
  the examples load; the screens only show them.
