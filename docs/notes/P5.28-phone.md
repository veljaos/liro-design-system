## 1. Decisions (for `docs/decisions.md`)

- **2026-10-13 — A phone frame is a phone's screen (P5.28 items 1, 5 and 11).** `PhoneFrame`
  (`packages/ui/src/components/story-frames.tsx`, stories only) is now the screen of a phone, not
  a clipping box: the frame (390 × 844, `data-liro-phone-frame`) has the transform that makes it
  the containing block of every fixed element inside it, and `overflow: clip`; inside it a scroll
  container (`data-liro-phone-page`, full height, scrollbar not drawn, `overscroll-behavior:
contain`) holds the page. The page scrolls inside the frame as on a phone — the sticky header
  and the sticky bottom bars stay in place, a long page can be read to its end — while fixed
  elements (backdrops, dialogs, sheets, the toaster) stay on the frame, because their containing
  block is the frame, outside the scrolling. A picture (the visual tests, a full-page screenshot)
  is still the frame's first screen, 390 × 844. **Cause of "Move early to… darkens only the top
  band" (item 5), found:** the frame had `overflow: hidden`; a hidden overflow can still be
  scrolled by the browser, and a dialog's focus brought an element into view by scrolling the
  whole frame, so the fixed backdrop — positioned in the frame — moved up with it and left the
  lower part of the screen undimmed (measured: the backdrop 109px above the frame in "Invoice,
  phone" after "Search…"). `overflow: clip` cannot be scrolled; the page scrolls in its own
  container. The automated check (below) asserts that every backdrop covers the whole screen.
- **2026-10-13 — The screen context (P5.28).** The frame tells the components inside that they
  are on a phone through an internal context (`packages/ui/src/provider/screen.ts`, not exported):
  `usePhone()` (and `useBelowMd()`) answer true inside a frame whatever the window's width, and
  every floating content — select lists, popovers, menus and submenus, tooltips — takes the
  frame as Radix's collision boundary, so it keeps inside the frame as it keeps inside the
  viewport on a real phone. Before, a component in a phone frame inside a wide window drew its
  desktop layout unless the story passed `layout="phone"`, and its floating lists measured the
  window, so they ran off the frame. In an application nothing sets the context: the viewport is
  the screen.
- **2026-10-13 — Floating content never runs off the screen (P5.28 item 1).** Rules in the
  primitives, for every user at once: a select list, popover, menu, submenu and tooltip keep
  8px inside the screen (`collisionPadding` 8, Mantine's default) and are never wider than the
  room there (`max-width` = Radix's available width); a long option, menu item or tooltip wraps
  (`overflow-wrap: anywhere`; the tooltip is one line as wide as its text up to that width). On
  a phone a popover or menu always moves to stay on the screen, even where a component chose a
  fixed side for a stable desktop picture (`avoidCollisions={false}`: LegalText's reference
  preview, CalendarView's day list, the legal search's sort menu). Width limits written in `vw`
  (`max-w-[calc(100vw-32px)]` in ListPage's view menu and the notifications panel,
  `calc(100vw-2rem)` in LookupField's list) measured the window, not the frame; they now follow
  the popover's own limit.
- **2026-10-13 — Dialogs on phones are bottom sheets (P5.28 item 1).** On a phone (`usePhone`)
  every Dialog, ConfirmDialog (and its presets) and any dialog built on the dialog or alert-dialog
  primitive is a sheet across the screen's full width on its bottom edge: as high as its content
  up to the screen's height less 40px (the page above shows it is a sheet over the page), radius
  lg on its top corners, sliding up 200ms, the bottom safe area as padding (`PHONE_DIALOG` in
  `primitives/classes.ts`). Its size ('wide') does not apply on a phone. The desktop dialog's
  `5dvh` / `90dvh` / `90vw` are now percentages of its containing block — the same on a page,
  and the frame (not the window) in a story. Drawers were already full width on a phone
  (`max-w-full`); LookupDialog and the company switcher stay full-screen sheets.
- **2026-10-13 — PeriodField on a phone (P5.28 item 1).** The popover (190px of presets beside
  a month) ran off a phone's screen ("Work-injury register, phone"). On a phone the field opens a
  sheet on the screen's bottom edge, titled by the field's label, with the close button: the
  presets first, in two columns of the same buttons ("Clear" under a line across both), then
  "Custom range" with one month across the sheet's full width (Calendar `fullWidth`: the seven
  columns share the width, the days keep their 36px height). The desktop popover is unchanged.
- **2026-10-13 — A long select is searched (P5.28 item 3).** `SelectField` with 11 options or
  more (`SEARCHABLE_FROM`, the owner's "more than about 10") is searched by typing, as Mantine's
  Select `searchable`: a text box showing the chosen label with the chevron at its end; a press
  or ArrowDown shows every option, typing filters them (ignoring case and accents, in the page's
  locale — `filterChoices`), the keys of ComboboxField choose (WAI-ARIA combobox: the focus stays
  in the box, `aria-activedescendant`), groups keep their headings (`role="group"`). Leaving the
  box puts the chosen label back; an emptied box clears the choice only when the field is
  `clearable`. `searchable` overrides the count either way. A list searched on the server stays
  ComboboxField. Chosen over making every application switch to ComboboxField: the value stays a
  string, the options and groups stay the same, and the pupil picker of AttendanceGrid's phone
  layout (30+ pupils) and every other long SelectField change with no code. SelectField also
  gets `defaultOpen` (P5.23: an open list is pictured directly).
- **2026-10-13 — Lists scroll with the finger (P5.28 item 3).** Radix's scroll-up and scroll-down
  buttons are gone from every select list (on touch they stood in the finger's way, and on a
  desktop they are not needed): the list scrolls natively — wheel, finger, keys — and shows a
  thin scrollbar (Radix hides the viewport's scrollbar for its buttons; `scrollbar-width: thin`
  brings it back). ComboboxField, MultiSelectField and SelectField's searched list open their
  list on a press (before, only typing or ArrowDown opened it).
- **2026-10-13 — LookupDialog on a phone: compact rows (P5.28 item 13).** On a phone the
  results are one compact row each — the title (default `getRowLabel`), `mobile.subtitle`
  ("code · kind") and `mobile.badge` at the end (the price) — and under it only the columns
  `mobile.details` names, **default none** (DataTable's default, every column, made each result
  a full detail card over the line form). The examples pass the price as the badge (Invoice
  draft, the specification; the bank statement's open items: number; partner · due date; open
  amount).
- **2026-10-13 — ActionGroup's measuring copy is clipped (P5.28 item 1).** ActionGroup lays every
  action out once, invisibly and unwrapped, to measure what fits. The copy (absolutely
  positioned, as wide as all actions) made a phone page scroll sideways (816px in a 390px frame
  on every legal act page). It now stands in a box of no height that clips it; the measuring is
  unchanged.
- **2026-10-13 — Long texts that made a phone page scroll sideways (P5.28 item 1, found by the
  check).** FilterBar's pills: a pill's text is cut with an ellipsis, but the list of pills, a
  flex item, kept the width of its longest pill (1,175px in "FilterBar / Long text, phone
  width"); the list and the pill's text may now shrink. Breadcrumbs: every crumb is one line;
  the current page's crumb (the last, never a link) longer than the row is now cut with an
  ellipsis (705px in "Navigation / Long text, phone width"); the links before it stay whole, so
  their focus ring is never clipped.
- **2026-10-13 — A long list keeps its active option in view (P5.28 item 3).** ComboboxField,
  MultiSelectField, SelectField's searched list (and LookupField's own list is unchanged) keep the
  focus in the text box, so the browser did not scroll the list as ArrowDown moved past its
  220px: the active option went out of sight. The list now scrolls itself (only the list, never
  the page) to keep the active option in view. A searched SelectField opens with its chosen
  option active, as Mantine's.
- **2026-10-13 — The phone check (P5.28 items 1 and 5).** `apps/storybook/tests/phone-overflow.spec.ts`
  (protected: a new test file under `apps/storybook/tests/`). Every story whose name says it is
  drawn on a phone (`/phone|handheld|mobile/i`: "…, phone", "Phone width", "Long text, phone
  width", the handheld examples) is opened in a window 456 × 900 (a phone's 390px with the
  story's margin and a phone-width story's own padding; below 48em, so the components lay
  themselves out as on a phone), light theme, left to right and right to left. The screen is the story's
  PhoneFrame, or the window when it draws none (the "Long text, phone width" stories with a
  390px column). Checked as drawn and then with every trigger opened one at a time —
  `aria-haspopup` (selects, menus, popovers, dialog triggers), comboboxes (a press, then
  ArrowDown), and buttons whose name ends in "…" (the convention of a button that opens a
  dialog) — and, in a dialog or sheet that opens, its own triggers (a select inside a dialog);
  a trigger repeated down a page (an article's or a row's menu) twice at most, 40 per screen:
  the page does not scroll sideways; every open overlay lies inside the screen and nothing inside
  it is wider than its box (a cut option; an ellipsis on purpose is allowed); every backdrop
  covers the whole screen. It runs with the story tests (`test:stories` in
  `apps/storybook/package.json`), so CI shards it with them; no workflow change.
