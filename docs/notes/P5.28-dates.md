## 1. Decision entries (for `docs/decisions.md`)

- **2026-10-13 — Day and month names, dates and the week's first day come only from the
  provider's locale (P5.28 item 2).** The owner saw an English interface with "Januar, Ned Pon
  Uto", "sre 7", "utorak, 6. oktobar 2026." and weeks from Sunday. **Root cause:** the examples'
  `ExampleProvider` (and screens-G's `Tenant`, and `shift-planner.test`) passed the story's
  locale ("en") with a whole Serbian `format` (`createFormat('sr-Latn-RS')`): `format` replaced
  `monthName`, `weekdayName` and `dateLong` with Serbian ones, while the week's first day came
  from "en" (Sunday). No component hardcodes a locale: the calendar primitive, CalendarView,
  ResourceSchedule, Timetable, SlotPicker, ShiftPlanner, MyShifts, AttendanceGrid, DateField,
  DateRangeField, MonthField and PeriodField all take their names from `format` and their week
  from `weekStartsOn` (checked, every one). **The rule:** month and weekday names, long dates and
  the week's first day come only from the provider's `locale` (the week from its region, or the
  provider's `weekStartsOn`, the tenant's setting); `format` may replace how _digits_ are written,
  never the words. **New helper `numericFormat(locale, timeZone?)`** (exported, unit-tested): the
  numbers, amounts, percentages and numeric dates and times of another locale without its words
  (no `monthName`, `weekdayName`, `dateLong`, `businessYear`). An English interface for a Serbian
  company is `locale="en-RS"` with `format={numericFormat('sr-Latn-RS')}`: "12.345,60 RSD",
  "06.10.2026.", "09:42", "Tuesday, October 6, 2026", weeks from Monday (the region RS). **The
  examples** now do exactly that: `ExampleProvider` uses `tenantLocale(story locale)` ("en" →
  "en-RS", "ar" → "ar-RS"; a locale with a region keeps it) and the tenant's digits;
  `EXAMPLE_FORMAT` (story-frames) builds the long dates the example checks expect; screens-G's
  `Tenant` passes `numericFormat('sr-Latn-RS', 'Europe/Belgrade')` and no longer forces
  `weekStartsOn={1}` (the locale's region gives Monday); `ScheduleFrame` (the scheduling stories)
  takes the week from `tenantLocale` instead of forcing Monday on an "en" locale. Stories (static):
  "Internal/Primitives/Calendar": English (United States) from Sunday, English for a Serbian company
  (en-RS) from Monday, Serbian (sr-Latn) from Monday with Serbian names and control labels;
  "Components/Scheduling/CalendarView": Month, English (United States) from Sunday; Month, Serbian
  (sr-Latn) from Monday with Serbian texts from `messages`.
- **2026-10-13 — Sticky table headers stick below the shell; the first column stays at the start
  (P5.28 item 4).** A wide grid scrolls sideways in its own region (`overflow-x: auto`), and such
  a region is a scroll container: a `position: sticky` header inside it sticks to the region,
  never to the page, so on a long page it scrolled away under the AppShell's header (School,
  gradebook: the dates row disappeared). CSS cannot make one element sticky to the page and
  scroll sideways in a box at once, so **one small mechanism** (`components/sticky-head.ts`,
  internal): the header cells carry `STICKY_HEAD` (`sticky top-0` inside their region, then
  `translate-y` by `--liro-head-shift`), and `useStickyHead(regionRef)` keeps `--liro-head-shift`
  on the region equal to how far the region's top has gone above the page's sticky edge — the
  bottom of the shell's sticky top (`--liro-shell-top`, published by AppShell) or the top of the
  scrolling container — clamped so the header never leaves its region (`headShift`, unit-tested).
  It listens to every scroll (document, capture: the window, a phone frame, an application's
  container), resize and the region's size, once a frame; the scrolling container is the nearest
  ancestor that scrolls vertically, else the page; the shell's height counts only when the shell
  scrolls in that container. **The rule for every table, grid and schedule with a header row:**
  (1) the header cells `STICKY_HEAD` (on an opaque surface), the header group `data-sticky-head`;
  (2) the first column (row headers: people, names, resources) `sticky start-0` on an opaque
  surface; the corner cell both, marked `data-sticky-start`; layers inside the region: body 0,
  first column 5, header 10, corner 20; (3) the region `STICKY_CLEARANCE`: a cell the keyboard
  reaches is scrolled clear of the shell, the header and the first column (scroll margins from
  `--liro-shell-top`, `--liro-head-height`, `--liro-start-width`, kept by the hook; WCAG 2.4.11);
  (4) anything else sticky at the top of a page (a phone's totals card) uses
  `top-[var(--liro-shell-top,0px)]`, never `top-0`. **Applied:** AttendanceGrid (timesheet,
  attendance, gradebook: the days row and the corner; people already sticky), ShiftPlanner (the
  days row and the corner get the raised surface; people already sticky, now under the header),
  ResourceSchedule (the days and hours rows as one sticky header; names already sticky),
  DataTable (`stickyHeader` now follows the page, no `maxHeight` needed; **in a card** — `inCard`,
  where the surface is known to be raised — the selection column and the first column stay at the
  start, rows get the raised surface so the sticky cells inherit hover and selection; totals' first
  cell too), EditableGrid (the header row always, its lines redrawn as shadows because the grid's collapsed borders stay where the cells were; in a card the first column, with its end line redrawn; on phones the totals
  card now sticks at `--liro-shell-top`, it slid under the shell), CalendarView (the day and week
  views' days row and the month's weekday row). RoomRack has no header row (rooms are tiles under
  floor headings; nothing sticky), Timetable's few periods need none. Stories that act (a picture
  cannot scroll): "Thirty … under the shell, interaction" in AttendanceGrid, ShiftPlanner,
  ResourceSchedule, DataTable and EditableGrid (plus EditableGrid's phone one), checking that the
  header (and the corner) stands right below a 56px shell bar after the page scrolled 300–400px
  past the grid's top, and that the first column stays while the grid scrolls sideways; and
  "Examples / School, gradebook, header under the shell, interaction" in the real AppShell. The
  examples' class 7/2 now has 30 pupils (the owner's gradebook), Luka still the fourth.
- **2026-10-13 — One "now" marker (P5.28 item 10).** CalendarView had none, the clinic schedule
  none, and Timetable outlined the running period and put a "Now" chip in it (it pushed the lesson
  down and looked like a button). Now one internal `NowMarker` (`components/now-marker.tsx`): a 2px
  line in border.selected with an 8px dot of the same colour at its start (the leading edge of a
  horizontal line, the top of a vertical one) — neutral, never blue (D17) and never a status
  colour. It is an image named "Now, 09:40" (`messages['schedule.now']`, the time through
  `format.time`). CalendarView (day and week; across today's column; its slot `aria-current=
"time"`), ResourceSchedule (desktop: down across the rows, under the names and the header;
  phone: across the track) and Timetable (across today's running period at its place in the
  period; the cell keeps `aria-current="time"`, no outline, no chip) draw it. The time comes from
  the application: a new `now` prop on CalendarView and ResourceSchedule ("HH:mm" of the
  provider's today, or an instant shown in the provider's time zone, or a local
  "YYYY-MM-DDTHH:mm"), Timetable's `now` reads the same forms; never `Date.now`, so pictures are
  stable (`nowMoment`, `nowOnAxis`, `nowWithin`, unit-tested). `timetable.now` is replaced by
  `schedule.now`. The clinic examples (schedule and doctor's calendar) are seen on the day of
  Jovana's appointment (`CLINIC.day`, 14.10.2026, as their today) at 09:40.

## 3. Changeset lines (@veljaos/ui)

- `numericFormat(locale, timeZone?)`: another locale's digits without its words. Month and
  weekday names, long dates and the week's first day come only from the provider's `locale`.
- CalendarView and ResourceSchedule take `now` (the application's clock) and draw the one "now"
  marker; Timetable draws the same marker in the running period instead of an outline and a
  "Now" chip. **Breaking (alpha):** message `timetable.now` removed; new `schedule.now`
  (`(timeText) => string`).
- Sticky table headers stay below the shell's sticky header while the page scrolls
  (AttendanceGrid, ShiftPlanner, ResourceSchedule, DataTable `stickyHeader` — no `maxHeight`
  needed —, EditableGrid, CalendarView); in a card, DataTable's and EditableGrid's first column
  stays at the start; EditableGrid's phone totals stick below the shell.
