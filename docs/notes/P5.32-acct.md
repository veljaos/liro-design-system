## 5. For the lead

- **Plan names differ between groups.** The brief and P5.36 say Start / Business / Pro; the
  Superadmin's tenants (`data-R.ts`, `TenantPlan`) use Starter / Business / Enterprise / Trial.
  Kvadrat Gradnja is "Business" in both. Suggest renaming R's to Start / Business / Pro / Trial.
- **Admin overview licences (screens-Q).** Its "24 of 30 seats used" + ProgressBar could become a
  `UsageMeter label="Seats" used="24" limit="30"` (same words, one look); and the overview's plan
  "Liro Business" could link to the new Subscription page (`Z_ROUTES.subscription`).
- **Admin tabs.** Group Z appends a "Subscription" tab to `adminTabs()` in its own shell
  (`billingTabs()` in screens-Z); `ADMIN_TAB_LIST` in screens-Q does not have it, so the other
  administration pages do not show the tab. Add `{ key: 'subscription', label: 'Subscription',
path: '/settings/subscription' }` to `ADMIN_TAB_LIST` (and `'subscription'` to `AdminTab`), then
  `billingTabs()` becomes `adminTabs('subscription')`.
- **`example-shell.tsx`**: `Shell` gained an optional `banners` prop (three lines, marked
  "P5.36 acct").
- **Kvadrat Gradnja's tax number.** `example-shell`'s COMPANIES and data-J/R give 108452317;
  the brief says 100421987 "as already in the data", which in `example-shell` is Vojvođanka Mlin
  a.d. Groups V and Z use 108452317 for Kvadrat Gradnja and 100421987 for Vojvođanka Mlin.
