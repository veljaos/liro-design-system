# P5.28 — POS helper's notes (items 21 and 22)

For the lead to fold into `docs/decisions.md`, `AGENTS.md` and `.changeset/`, then delete.

## 1. Decisions (for `docs/decisions.md`, section "Liro patterns (Phase 5 part 2)" or a new "P5.28" section)

- **2026-10-13 — Fiscal sale flows as data (P5.28 item 21).** The Serbian fiscal system (the
  electronic fiscal device and the processor of fiscal receipts) is the reference, but nothing of
  it lives in a component (D1, D2, D5): the kinds of a receipt, the ways to pay, the buyer's ID
  kinds, the counters, the markers, the rules ("a wire transfer needs the buyer's tax number",
  "a refund paid out in cash is signed by the buyer") and every printed word are the application's
  data. The stories and the examples carry an **illustrative** English rendering
  (`packages/ui/src/components/sale-story-data.ts`, `apps/storybook/src/examples/data-I-fiscal.ts`):
  invented processor numbers (`ZK7Q2M4P-ZK7Q2M4P-2318`), invented tax and personal numbers, the
  counter's suffixes written after the system's two letters. The receipt's legally prescribed
  lines are printed in the application's language ("FISCAL RECEIPT", "THIS IS NOT A FISCAL
  RECEIPT", "END OF FISCAL RECEIPT" in the English examples). The components compute nothing
  (D4): the remaining amount, the change, a refund's amount, the advances deducted, promotions,
  discounts, shares and the day's totals arrive as decimal strings.
- **2026-10-13 — The kind of a receipt: type × transaction (item 21a).** `ReceiptKindPicker`: two
  groups of 44px pressed buttons (the neutral selection, never blue), the application's types
  (Normal, Advance, Proforma, Copy, Training) and transactions (Sale, Refund); a kind that cannot
  be chosen here stays focusable with its reason ("Copy: open the receipt in the journal to copy
  it."); the chosen kind's description and **marker** (`ReceiptKindOption.marker`) in a warning
  Alert. A kind that is not a real sale is marked three times, all with the application's words:
  the picker, the cart (`CartPanel` `kindLabel` under the title, `marker` as a warning Alert above
  the lines) and the receipt (`ReceiptPreview` `marker`, printed boxed and bold under the facts
  and again above the footer; the repeat is `aria-hidden`). **Training** adds a persistent warning
  `Banner` in `PosShell`'s new `banners` slot (role status, "Leave training" neutral). Choosing
  Refund in the picker starts from the original receipt (the application's rule in the example).
- **2026-10-13 — Several ways to pay on one receipt (item 21b).** `PaymentDialog` takes the
  application's own ways to pay: `methods` may hold `PaymentOption`s `{ id, label, kind, icon?,
unavailableReason?, note?, referenceLabel? }` beside the P5.10 names; `kind` is `cash` (quick
  amounts, keypad, change), `card` (a terminal's states), `qr` (an instant payment: the
  application's code shown large — `qrCode` — with the same four states as a card, its own words)
  or `plain` (an amount and, when asked, a reference: a cheque number, a voucher's code). Up to four
  ways stay a segmented row; more wrap as a grid of 44px pressed buttons (minmax 8.5rem; the neutral
  selection). **With `onAddPart` several ways pay one sale:** every panel takes "Amount of this
  payment" (the keypad) and "Add this payment"; a card or a code adds its part when the
  application says it is approved; the parts paid (`paid`, each with an optional `detail` and, with
  `onRemovePart`, a 44px remove button) and the remaining amount stand on top; "Exact amount" is
  the remaining amount once parts are paid (no arithmetic: the application's figure). A way that
  cannot be used yet (`unavailableReason`, e.g. a deferred payment without the buyer) can still be
  chosen: its panel shows the reason in a warning Alert, and the application's `extra` content
  (the BuyerField) under it. `doneContent` takes the issued receipt (ReceiptResult). The old
  `split` view and the P5.10 API stay as they were; `onAddPart` now reports `(method: string,
amount)` and `method` / `onMethodChange` take strings (an option's id). The example offers the
  seven ways of the system: cash, card, instant payment, cheque, voucher (single-purpose; a
  multi-purpose one is "Other cashless" — the application's note), wire transfer (deferred, buyer
  required), other cashless.
- **2026-10-13 — The buyer (item 21c).** `BuyerField`: a `fieldset` named by its legend (" *" when
  the application's rule requires it, the rule as the description), the ID's kind and the ID side
  by side in a FormGrid (36rem), the ID left to right in every language; an optional second field
  with its kind when the application gives `optionalTypes`; a kind's `hint` ("9 digits") under the
  ID; the application's checks as `errors`. **More than ten kinds become a searchable
  ComboboxField** (`BUYER_TYPES_SEARCHABLE_FROM` 11, item 3's rule); the example's fifteen kinds
  are searchable. The corporate card is the buyer (the company's tax number) with the card's last
  digits in the optional field, paid by card.
- **2026-10-13 — Refunds: find, choose, carry over (item 21d).** `ReceiptFinder`: the ScanField
  first ("Scan the receipt's code"; the camera through `onCameraScan`), then "Or search by number,
  date or total" (a FormGrid of three fields, Search neutral); the application searches and
  answers with `FoundReceipt`s as rows (CandidateList's look: number left to right, date and time,
  the kind, a detail, the total; chosen = the neutral selection; a receipt refunded in full stays
  focusable with its reason); skeletons while searching, the no-results EmptyState, the error in a
  danger Alert. `LineQuantityPicker` (one pattern for a partial refund, a split bill, paying part
  and moving items): "All lines" tri-state, a checkbox per line (ticked = the whole quantity
  available), a QuantityStepper 0 … available, "of 3 pc", the application's note ("1 of 4 refunded
  already"), a line that cannot be chosen with its reason, and the application's figure for what
  is chosen (`chosenTotal`, settling). The refund in the cart is "Normal · Refund", read-only lines,
  "To pay out", "Pay out 240,00 RSD"; its receipt carries the original's number and time
  (facts), the buyer, and a **signature line only where the application's rule asks**
  (`ReceiptPreview` `signature`; the example: paid out in cash). Logic `sale-logic.ts` (tested):
  `isChosen`, `chosenLineIds`, `toggleLine`, `chooseAllLines`, `allLinesChosen`,
  `exceedsAvailable` — quantities compared exactly as decimal strings.
- **2026-10-13 — Advances (item 21e).** `AdvanceList`: a buyer's open advances (number left to
  right, the day paid, the state as a badge in the application's tone, the note, "Open" and "of …
  paid"), choosable with `onSelectionChange` (checkbox "Deduct advance …", the neutral selection),
  a refund button per advance (`onRefund`, "Refund advance …"), the application's "Deducted"
  figure; loading, empty, error. In the example the buyer's advances stand in the cart's
  `details`; one deducted, the rest "To pay" (`CartPanel` `totalLabel`). Taking an advance is a
  sale of the Advance kind (buyer, amount, what it is for); refunding one asks first
  (ConfirmDialog: the action as the title, the advance bold on its own line, item 6).
- **2026-10-13 — Cancel, copy, without compensation, corporate card (item 21f).** Not components
  but the application's flows on the components: cancelling a receipt issued by mistake is a
  ReasonConfirmDialog (the application's reasons) that issues a refund of the whole receipt with
  its number and time; a copy is the receipt printed as the Copy kind with the marker; a sale
  without compensation is an option of the kind dialog (the application's rule: paid only as
  Other cashless, printed "WITHOUT COMPENSATION"); a corporate card is the buyer plus the card.
- **2026-10-13 — The result, the journal, the daily report (item 21g).** `ReceiptResult`: the
  outcome (a success check "Receipt issued", or with `tone="waiting"` a neutral CloudUpload
  "Receipt issued, waiting to be sent" — never blue), the application's facts (processor number
  and time, the counter) as rows left to right, the code on a raised tile beside them from 30rem
  (container query; under them on phones), the link (left to right, new tab), and **delivery**:
  a row per way (print, e-mail, SMS / link), its own address field (e-mail / telephone, left to
  right), a neutral action named by the way ("Send by e-mail"), and its state in words with an
  icon (a spinner "Sending…", sent, failed in danger with the application's words); `onDeliver(id,
address)`. `ReceiptPreview` prints the fiscal parts as data: `banner` and `end` between double
  rules (no more "=====" text in data), `kindLine` between dashed rules, `marker`, a `recap` table
  (columns and rows from the application; amount columns written by `format`), `issuedFacts` after
  it, `signature`. The journal is a DataTable inside PosShell (the list in the product area, the
  chosen receipt as printed in the cart area; on phones the receipt first and "Receipts" opens the
  list): time, number, kind, total, state; Copy, Refund…, Cancel receipt… The daily report is a
  ReceiptPreview (sales, refunds, net, by way of paying, the tax per label; the kinds that are not
  in the totals named in the footer).
- **2026-10-13 — Offline and its queue (item 21h).** `PosShell` `queueState` ('sending' default, a
  neutral badge; 'failed', a danger "Not sent" badge — `pos.queueFailed`) and `queueAction` (the
  example: "Show receipts" opens the journal of the receipts not sent). A receipt issued offline
  has the till's local number and "Waiting to be sent" in the journal; refused ones say why, with
  "Send again" in a danger Banner (its action neutral, item 7).
- **2026-10-13 — An online sale (item 21i).** A back-office page of the web-shop order WEB-1042:
  the order, and "Fiscal receipt" — ReceiptResult "Issued and e-mailed" with the processor's
  facts, the code, the link and the e-mail sent; "Show receipt" opens it as printed.
- **2026-10-13 — Open tabs on tables (P5.28 item 22).** A table's tab is the application's record
  and outlives the screen: the examples keep it above the screens (`useExampleState`,
  `apps/storybook/src/examples/example-state.tsx`, provided by ExampleApp) — the waiter adds items,
  goes back to the terrace (the plan shows the tab's time and what it still has to pay) and comes
  back to the same tab. On the tab (`screens-K-tabs.tsx`, one screen for Kafić Kod Mike and Bistro
  Dunav): each line's state in words (`CartPanel` line `state`: "Sent to the bar · 18:15", "Not
  sent yet" in the warning tone, "Paid"), its note (`note`), an edit button (`onEditLine`: the
  application's dialog — quantity, a note for the kitchen or the bar, a discount from the venue's
  list, Remove line); "Send order (n)" (primary, secondary weight) sends the unsent lines to their
  stations ("Sent: 2 to the bar, 1 to the kitchen."); the promotions from the venue's data
  (Espresso 3 for 2; a juice hour; the bistro's dessert of the day) and the waiter's discounts as
  the line's discount text and total rows; a discount on the whole order; **Split the bill**
  (equally: People, the application's shares, "Pay one share") or **Pay part** (by items:
  LineQuantityPicker, the application's amount); **Move…** the whole tab or some items to another
  table (free, or an open tab that is joined; more than ten tables a searchable combobox); **Bill**
  closes it with the fiscal receipt (ReceiptResult) and "Close table". The bistro's table panel
  gains "Tab" (and the tab's rest to pay); every figure is computed in `data-K-tabs.ts`.
- **2026-10-13 — What the application keeps outlives the screen (class of item 22).** In the
  examples, records the user changes on a screen and expects to find again are kept above the
  screens with `useExampleState(key, initial)`: the tabs, the till's sale and journal, the open
  advances, the pick list and the stock count (the count is reached from the pick list and returns
  to it). Pictures still start from their route's state.
