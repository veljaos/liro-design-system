# P5.82–P5.84 notes (helper "kyc"): client risk assessment, know your customer, screening

Group E, first part. Examples only: every screen is built from the library as it is. **No new
component, no new prop, no new message key, no dependency.**

## 1. Decision entries (for `docs/decisions.md`)

- **2026-10-16 — AML compliance is assembled, not built (P5.82–P5.84).** The client risk
  assessment, the client file and the screening are made of DetailPage, ListPage, WorklistPage,
  Questionnaire, ReasonConfirmDialog, DropdownMenu, KeyValueList, KeyFigures, DataTable,
  FilterBar, SidePanels, AttachmentList, HierarchyChart, MaskedValue, HistoryList,
  ValueComparison, StatusBadge, DueDate and Alert. Nothing was missing, so nothing was added.
  Every risk, factor, effect, point, score, band, review cycle, next review, share, document
  verdict, PEP status, list, entry, match score, comparison mark and consequence is the
  application's data; the screens and the library never score, match, rank or decide, they show
  the application's figures and words and report the user's decisions with the reason the
  application requires.
- **2026-10-16 — A risk is a word in a tone (P5.82).** Low, Medium and High stand as StatusBadges
  ("Medium risk") in success, warning and danger — the word always carries the meaning. A
  factor's effect is the application's word ("Higher risk" in warning, "Standard" and "Lower
  risk" neutral) beside its points as the application writes them ("+20", "0"); the factors'
  table ends with the application's score as its total row ("Score 40") — given, never summed.
  The score is written "40 of 100" with its band in the application's words ("Medium: 30–59
  points").
- **2026-10-16 — An override keeps the assessment beside it (P5.82).** "Override risk" (a neutral
  DropdownMenu button) offers the levels other than the one in force ("Set to High"; "Back to
  Medium, the assessment's" when an override is in force); the choice is confirmed in
  ReasonConfirmDialog — title "Override risk", the client as `subject`, the assessment's result
  and the consequence in the message, the application's reasons (one required) and optional
  details, the caution family. The page then shows an "Override" section: by the assessment,
  set to, reason, set by and when, details; the history records the change (Risk: Medium →
  High). The assessment's own level is never hidden.
- **2026-10-16 — A review starts from the last answers (P5.82).** The risk review is the
  Questionnaire with the application's questions and branch (the owners above a holding are asked
  about only when a company owns the client), the last assessment's answers filled in to be
  confirmed or changed, the summary where any answer can be changed, and "Send for assessment";
  the application's result opens on the assessment page.
- **2026-10-16 — Next review by DueDate, document validity by the application's verdict (P5.82,
  P5.83).** The next review is DueDate with `warningDays={30}` (its "N days overdue" reads right
  for a review). A document's validity is the application's verdict as AttachmentList's `flag`
  ("Expired" danger, "Expires soon" and "Older than 12 months" warning; nothing for a valid one)
  with "valid until …" or "issued …" in the row's detail — not DueDate, whose words are a
  payment's. The "Review due" and "Needs documents" views follow the application's flags (the
  file's state), not dates the screen compares.
- **2026-10-16 — Owners as a tree, beneficial owners as a list (P5.83).** HierarchyChart draws the
  client on top and each owner under what it owns, the share on the line (`lineLabel`); a person
  is marked "Beneficial owner" by the application's rule (its words, under the section's title),
  a PEP or a potential PEP match as a badge in words; owners the client has not disclosed are a
  vacant node with `vacantLabel` "Unknown owner". Under the chart a DataTable lists the beneficial
  owners with the share in the application's words ("Indirectly 70%"), country and PEP status.
- **2026-10-16 — Identity numbers are masked, a match's facts are not (P5.83, P5.84).** In the
  client file the representative's national ID number and date of birth are MaskedValues (the
  application's reasons and "Other", "Access is logged" with what is kept). In a screening match
  the date of birth is shown beside the listed one, because the decision rests on it and only the
  compliance officer reviews matches.
- **2026-10-16 — A screening match side by side (P5.84).** WorklistPage, one match at a time: the
  screened person or company with its client (a link to the file), the list with its version,
  the application's match score ("74 of 100"), the listed entry, then ValueComparison with the
  columns "Client's record" and the list's name, each line marked by the application (`differs`,
  `note`: "Same, without diacritics", "Different", "Not on the list") and "3 of 4 differ" above
  it, and the application's summary under it. While a match is undecided the detail starts with
  the confidentiality reminder (below). Decisions: "False positive…" (neutral, CircleSlash) and
  "True match…" (caution, ShieldAlert), neither filled — a match has no default answer; each asks
  ReasonConfirmDialog for the application's reasons; the true match's message is the
  application's consequence ("The owner becomes a politically exposed person: …"). Several
  matches can be marked false positive at once (BulkActionBar), never confirmed as true matches
  together. After a decision the next match opens; on a phone the list comes first and the
  decisions stand in the bottom bar.
- **2026-10-16 — The client must not be told (P5.84; for P5.85–P5.88).** Wherever a potential
  match, a suspicion or a report is shown, a warning Alert titled "Confidential" says in the
  application's words that the client must not be informed (`ConfidentialNote` in the examples'
  `aml-shared.tsx`): never colour alone, never only an icon.
- **2026-10-16 — Screening results per person and list (P5.84).** A client's screening page lists
  the client, its owners and its representative against every list (one column per list): "No
  match" in words, a decided match as its badge, an undecided one as "Potential match" with a
  "Review" link to it; the lists with their versions; the re-screening history (HistoryList, the
  application's runs as a system actor, the decisions as people's entries). "Screen again" only
  asks the application.

## New components and why

None. Considered and not needed:

- A "risk level" component: StatusBadge with the application's word and tone says it.
- A "match comparison" component: ValueComparison (P5.33) already lays two sources side by side
  with the application's marks.
- A risk-override form: ReasonConfirmDialog after a DropdownMenu choice of the level is one
  decision with a required reason; a Drawer form would add a container for two fields.
- CandidateList was not used: each potential match is decided on its own (True match / False
  positive), not chosen among candidates.
