- **2026-10-13 — StatutoryFormPage is the form's real grid (P5.28 item 19, the owner: "as the
  real form grid, like PP PDV, POPDV and the input-VAT records on ePorezi").** Replaces P5.20's
  one-table-per-section model (`StatutoryField` with `value` / `previous` is removed; alpha
  breaking). The model lives in `templates/statutory-logic.ts` (pure, tested in
  `statutory-logic.test.ts`):
  1. **Parts** (`StatutorySection`: `key`, `title`, `description`, `columns`, `items`) are cards,
     each with **its own columns** (`StatutoryColumn`: `id`, `label`, optional `group`).
     Neighbouring columns with the same `group` share one header above their own (two header
     rows, `statutoryHeaderRows`); a column without a group spans both rows; a one-column part
     ("Износ / вредност") has one header row.
  2. **Nested numbered groups** (`StatutoryGroup`: `key`, `number`, `title`, `items`) to any
     depth (Part 1 → 1.1 → 1.1.1). Every part and group is **collapsible** (a disclosure button
     with `aria-expanded`; a part's in its h2); a collapsed group's rows are **not drawn**
     (`statutoryGridLines`). `defaultCollapsed` (keys) for the first view. "Go to" opens the
     part and the groups around its target first (`findStatutoryPlace` → `path`), then scrolls
     to and focuses the row or cell.
  3. **Rows** (`StatutoryRow`: `id`, `number`, `label`, `cells` by column id, `total`). **A
     column without a cell does not apply: its place is blank** — never 0, never a disabled
     field (the owner: "1.3 and 1.4 have Base only, 1.1.5 has VAT only"). In the grid the empty
     cell carries `data-cell-kind="absent"`; on phones it is left out.
  4. **Three kinds of cells** (`StatutoryCell.kind`): `entered` — the application's field
     (`field`, a MoneyField with its label hidden and named by `statutoryCellLabel`, e.g.
     "1.1.2 · Општа стопа 20% · Основица"), plain text when `readOnly`; `computed` — read-only
     text (a total); `source` — read-only, the value a link-coloured button (dotted underline)
     that opens its documents in an end Drawer (`sources`, `sourceName` such as "Електронске
     фактуре из СЕФ-а" heads the list). A computed or source cell with `editor` can be typed over
     (P5.20's override: pencil, Cancel / Save, "Changed manually" with who, when, the computed
     value and "Use computed value"); the row shows the badge with the cell's column and the way
     back (in a one-column part without the column's name).
  5. **Ids:** a cell is `statutoryCellId(row, column)` ("1.1.2/base20"); rules' `fields`, the
     `editing` state, `onSaveOverride` and `onUseComputed` use row or cell ids. A rule shows under
     its row when it names the row or any of its cells (`statutoryRowRules`, failures first);
     "Go to" names the row's number.
  6. **The previous period is a comparison, never a replacement of the columns:** with
     `previousLabel` a switch "Compare with August 2026" (`compare` / `defaultCompare` /
     `onCompareChange`) shows each cell's `previous` under its value, xs secondary, its period
     named for assistive technology ("August 2026:"), and the note "September 2026, with August
     2026 under each amount" beside the switch. Two extra columns per rate were rejected: four
     rate columns would become eight.
  7. **Language of the form (D15):** `lang` ("sr-Cyrl") puts `lang` on the form's own texts —
     part and group titles, row labels, column headers, the phone's value labels — when it
     differs from the page's (`useLangAttribute`); the application's UI texts (checks, buttons)
     stay in the page's language.
  8. **Phones** (`layout="phone"` / `usePhone()`): each part a card on surface.sunken, its group
     headings as disclosure buttons, **one card per row** (number and label, its checks and
     overrides), each value labelled with its group and column ("Општа стопа 20% · Основица"),
     an entered cell's field full width under its label. No table on phones.
  9. Kept from P5.20: LifecycleBar, PageHeader with back / status / actions, the checks' summary
     Alert (compact with a title only), `notice`, the drill-down Drawer, overrides, `readOnly`.
     Messages: `statutory.compareWith`, `statutory.comparing`, `statutory.previousValue`

- **2026-10-13 — The statutory grid's sticky header and first column (P5.28 item 4, the
  owner's rule applied to the new grid).** The header rows (`thead`) are sticky at
  `top-[var(--liro-shell-top,0px)]`, z `--liro-layer-sticky`, so they stay under the AppShell's
  sticky header while the page scrolls; the cards around them have no `overflow` (SectionCard's
  `overflow-hidden` would have trapped the sticky header, so the parts draw their own card). The
  first column (number and label, `th scope="row"`) is `sticky start-0` on surface.raised. **A
  sideways scroller and a page-level sticky header cannot be combined in CSS** (an element that
  scrolls sideways is the sticky header's scroll container on both axes), so the grid measures
  itself (ResizeObserver): while it fits its card it does not scroll and its header sticks under
  the shell; when it does not fit, the grid becomes a named, focusable region (`role="region"`,
  the part's title, `tabIndex=0`) that scrolls both ways, at most the viewport's height under the
  shell (`max-h-[calc(100dvh-var(--liro-shell-top,0px)-2rem)]`), its header sticky at its own
  top and its first column sticky at the start. Group headings stay at the start while scrolling
  sideways (`--liro-grid-width`). Tables use `border-separate` (collapsed borders do not move with
  a sticky header). Story: "Narrow, scrolls sideways".

- **2026-10-13 — Electronic signature fields with their place in the PDF (P5.28 item 20).**
  Signature lines only where a signature is really required (a certificate, a report card, a
  finding), never on a business document. Each is a **`PrintSignatureField`** (`id` — stable,
  the same on every print —, `label`, `name`, `detail`, `image` once signed): the label xs grey,
  a **64 × 18mm** place on a thin border.strong line (`data-signature-field="<id>"`), the name
  and the detail under it; never split between pages. `PrintSignature.key` became `id` (alpha
  breaking). **The place on paper:** PrintPage measures every `[data-signature-field]` of its
  sheet after layout and on every resize (ResizeObserver), in millimetres as shares of the sheet's
  own width (the screen's `zoom` cancels out), and converts it with
  **`signatureFieldPlacement(id, box, area)`** (tested): the page body runs from the top margin
  and the repeated header (`area.top`, measured) to the bottom margin and the footer's room
  (`area.bottom`); the screen's long sheet is cut into pages of that height; a field that would
  cross a page's end starts the next page (`break-inside: avoid`); the result is the page (from
  1. and the rectangle in **PDF points, origin at the page's bottom-left** (A4 595.28 ×
     841.89pt; `PT_PER_MM` = 72 / 25.4, two decimals). It is written on the element
     (`data-pdf-page`, `data-pdf-rect="x y width height"`) and reported through
     **`onSignatureFields(fields)`** on PrintPage and every template (only when it changes). Exact
     for a field on the first page (the certificate, a one-page finding) and wherever the content
     before it breaks between lines; a row or heading kept whole on paper moves what follows a few
     millimetres down, so a tool placing a signature on a later page should treat the rectangle as
     the field's place and look there. **Signing on screen:** with `onSign` an empty field is a
     "Sign here" button (`messages['print.signHere']`, link colour, a dashed border.default box,
     described by the field's label, left off the paper) that reports the field's id; the
     application signs and passes the visible signature back as `image`. Margins in `mm`, `cm`,
     `in`, `pt` or `px` are read by `lengthToMm`. Messages: `print.amountDue`, `print.signHere`.
