# P5.30 d and e: the customer portal and the knowledge base (helper "portal", examples T)

Branch `p6/portal`. The lead folds these notes into `docs/decisions.md`, `AGENTS.md` and a
changeset, then deletes this file.

## Decisions

- **2026-10-14 — Six help-centre building blocks, generic (P5.30 d, e).** The library had no
  rating scale, no "Was this helpful?", no proposals while typing, no contents for one long page,
  no search result list outside the legal search and no category cards outside the Launchpad.
  Each is generic (no "ticket", "article" or "support" inside; every word is the application's or
  a provider message), in `packages/ui/src/components/`, with its logic in `help-logic.ts`
  (unit-tested), stories under "Components/Help/…" (default, every state, long text, phone,
  Arabic, Japanese, an interaction) and its exports in one block of `index.ts`.
- **2026-10-14 — SatisfactionRating (P5.30 d).** One question on a scale after work was done for
  the user. The scale is a radio group named by the question (Field `group`): Tab enters it, the
  arrows (following the page's direction) move and choose. Each step is a tile with the radio,
  the value (h3 size, semibold, tabular, digits through `format.number`) and the step's words
  (xs text.secondary): a step is never told by a colour or a face alone, and no step is an
  emoji. The whole tile is the label, at least 44px high; the chosen tile is the neutral
  selection (surface.selected, border.selected), only the radio's dot is blue (D17). Desktop:
  the steps in equal columns; phones: one row per step. An optional comment (TextAreaField
  "Comment (optional)"), Send (primary filled: the rating is the main action where it is shown).
  Send without a step says "Choose one of the answers." under the question instead of sending.
  `onSubmit` may return a promise ("Sending…" beside Send while it runs); `error` is the
  application's danger Alert above Send. Sent: a polite status with a success CircleCheck, the
  thanks and "Your answer: 4, Satisfied" with the comment. Controlled (`value`, `commentValue`,
  `submitted`) or not. Disabled with a visible reason.
- **2026-10-14 — HelpfulFeedback (P5.30 e).** "Was this helpful?" as a named group of two toggle
  buttons (`aria-pressed`), "Yes" with ThumbsUp and "No" with ThumbsDown — words beside the icons;
  the pressed one is the neutral selection, and the answer can be changed. Then a polite thanks.
  After No with `askWhy`: "What was missing?" and a neutral Send (`onComment`, a promise waits).
  `counts` is the application's sentence ("41 of 46 readers found this helpful").
- **2026-10-14 — LinkSuggestions (P5.30 d).** Pages the application proposes while the user
  writes (articles while a customer writes a ticket). The application searches (after a pause,
  300ms as ComboboxField) and gives them; the box shows them and never takes the focus, opens
  anything or blocks the form. A raised box with a border (not a toned background: a hint, not a
  warning), the application's title with a Lightbulb, each suggestion a link with an optional
  context line and a summary (two lines; the whole summary is the link's description). While
  searching, the old suggestions stay with "Looking for suggestions…". Without suggestions
  nothing is drawn but a hidden polite status, so "3 suggestions" / "No suggestions" is announced
  as they change. `onOpen` reports a press (an application may keep the draft or open a drawer).
- **2026-10-14 — PageContents and RichText `headingIds` (P5.30 e).** "On this page" for one long
  reading page: the headings as links (subsections indented), the current one neutral
  (text.primary medium, a border.selected start line, `aria-current="location"`; never blue: it
  is a place, not an action), following the scroll (`currentEntry`: the last heading above the
  line under `--liro-shell-top`). A press scrolls (smoothly unless reduced motion), focuses the
  heading and keeps it current until the scroll ends — the SectionBar's behaviour. Desktop: a
  list for the side column (`sticky` under the shell); phones: a disclosure, closed unless
  `defaultOpen`. `RichText` gained `headingIds="<prefix>"`: every top-level h2 and h3 gets a
  stable id from its words (`richTextHeadings(value, prefix)` in the rich-text subpath lists the
  same ids, `headingSlug` keeps letters and digits of any script), `tabIndex={-1}` and a scroll
  margin of `--liro-shell-top` + 16px (WCAG 2.4.11). Which one: SectionBar for a detail page's
  sections, LegalContents for a legal act, TreeView for a structure to browse.
- **2026-10-14 — SearchResultList (P5.30 e).** What a search across texts found, as a list (a
  snippet needs the width). LegalSearchPage's snippet idea fits and is reused: the parts are the
  same shape (`HighlightPart` = `LegalSnippetPart`), so `snippetFromRanges` makes them; each match
  is a `<mark>` in semibold text.primary on the warning background, in the title and the snippet;
  the link's name is the whole title. Over the list a polite count ("3 results") or the
  application's sentence; while searching again a spinner and "Updating…", the old results kept;
  skeletons at first; EmptyState's no-results or the application's `empty`; ErrorState with
  "Try again". LegalSearchPage keeps its own row (gazette, status, article link); the two could
  share `HighlightedText` later.
- **2026-10-14 — CategoryGrid (P5.30 e).** The categories of a collection as cards, the
  Launchpad's card look without its keys (1–9 are window-wide shortcuts, wrong on a help page) and
  editing: icon square (blue on hover), the count at the end in the application's words, the
  title and a description (three lines). The link is named by the title and described by the
  count and the description. Columns of at least 15rem as many as fit; one on phones.
- **2026-10-14 — The customer portal and the knowledge base (P5.30 d, e; examples T).** Built
  from PortalShell, PageHeader, SectionCard, RadioGroupField, TextField, TextAreaField,
  FileDropzone, AttachmentList, DataTable (cards on phones), KeyValueList, MessageThread,
  RichText and the six blocks above. Ticket statuses in words and tone, each with a hint for the
  customer: Open (info, "With Liro support"), Replied (warning, "Waiting for your answer"), Paused
  (neutral), Resolved (success, "Solved: rate the help you got"), Closed (neutral). A resolved
  ticket shows "Rate the help you got" above the conversation; a reply opens it again; a closed
  ticket's reply field is disabled with its reason. The new ticket's articles stand beside the
  form on a desktop (18rem column) and between the description and the files on phones; the
  draft is kept when the customer opens a proposed article and comes back. The agents' knowledge
  base is the same pages inside AppShell (Nikola Ilić, "Helpdesk › Knowledge base"), with each
  article's author, how helpful readers found it and "Copy link". The portal never receives the
  agents' internal notes: the dataset has none for it.

## For the lead

1. **Shared files**, appended at the end under `P5.29 portal`: `messages.ts`, `messages.en.ts`
   (17 keys: `rating.*`, `helpful.*`, `suggestions.*`, `pageContents.title`, `searchResults.*`),
   `provider-p15.test.tsx` (the keys inserted in their sorted places), `index.ts`,
   `rich-text/index.ts`, `example-app.tsx` (one import and one spread), `examples.stories.tsx`.
   `rich-text/rich-text.tsx` changed (the `headingIds` prop); the agent helper's composer uses
   RichTextField, so a conflict there is unlikely.
2. **Ticket numbers trip `liro/no-raw-colors`.** "#4821" is four hex digits after "#", so the
   rule reads it as a colour. data-T.ts keeps numbers without the sign and writes them with
   `ticketRef(number)` (a template literal), and the stories avoid "#" + digits in literals. The
   agent helper will meet the same; a rule exception in `eslint.config.mjs` (e.g. for
   `apps/storybook/src/examples/**`) or a shared `ticketRef` is the lead's choice.
3. **The shared facts.** `data-T.ts` copies the agents, tickets 4821–4823 and the three shared
   articles exactly as the brief gives them (`SHARED_TICKETS`, `AGENTS`, `ARTICLES`). One point
   in the brief does not add up: 4821's first response is due 10:30 and marked overdue, but "now"
   is 09:40. data-T keeps `overdue: true` as given; the portal does not show SLA times. The
   portal's tones (Open info, Replied warning, Paused neutral, Resolved success, Closed neutral)
   are in `TICKET_TONES`; unify them with the agents' inbox if theirs differ.
4. **Routes.** The portal is under `/support/…`; the agents' knowledge base under
   `/helpdesk/kb/…` (home, category, article, search). If the agent helper's AppShell for the
   helpdesk has module tabs (Inbox, Dashboard, Knowledge base), point its "Knowledge base" tab at
   `/helpdesk/kb` and pass the same tabs to `AgentFrame` in screens-T (it uses the example
   `Shell` with an `account` for Nikola Ilić and breadcrumbs only). The agents' reply composer
   can link to `T_ROUTES.kbArticle(slug)` / the article titles in `ARTICLES`.
5. Pictures: no baselines generated or committed.
