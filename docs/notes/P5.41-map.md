## 1. Decisions (for `docs/decisions.md`)

- **2026-10-14 — Maps on Leaflet 1.9.4, `@veljaos/ui/map` (P5.41, owner: "add maps … show lines around that municipality, or … geolocation confirmation; use open source systems").** Leaflet 1.9.4 (BSD-2-Clause, published 2023-05-18, no dependencies), pinned exactly, a runtime dependency of `@veljaos/ui` imported only by the new subpath (tsup entry `map`, `exports["./map"]`; only `dist/map.js` imports `leaflet`; consumer-check renders both components from the packed tarball, `apps/consumer-check/src/MapExample.tsx`). `@types/leaflet` 1.9.22 (MIT) is a development dependency. **Never react-leaflet** (Hippocratic License 2.1, not open source): `map/map-canvas.tsx` is our own thin wrapper on Leaflet's API. **Never MapLibre GL** here: WebGL pictures are not stable. Leaflet draws with **SVG** (`renderer: L.svg()`), with every animation off (zoom, fade, inertia), so a fitted view and its picture are the same every time.
  1. **Leaflet's style sheet, controls and images are not shipped.** `map/map.css` keeps the structural rules of `dist/leaflet.css` (panes, tiles, the SVG layer, touch actions; BSD-2-Clause, noticed) with tokens for every colour; imported into the base layer of `styles.css` (no hex, rgb or hsl). Shapes take their colours from CSS classes (CSS wins over the attributes Leaflet writes), so they follow the theme. Zoom in, zoom out and "Show all" are our IconButtons with lucide icons, at the page's end corner over the map. Leaflet's attribution control is off; the **tiles' attribution, from the application, is always written under the map** when tiles are given.
  2. **No backend (D1).** The base map is optional — `tiles?: { url, attribution, urlDark? }`, the application's (its own tile server, or OpenStreetMap's with its attribution); without tiles only the shapes are drawn on `surface.sunken`. Shapes are GeoJSON (`Polygon` / `MultiPolygon`, [longitude, latitude], holes after the outer ring). **The library never calls `navigator.geolocation`**: "Use my location" calls the application's `onLocate` (a returned promise shows "Finding your location…"), which asks the browser and passes back `{ lat, lng, accuracy, at }`. Stories and examples pass no tiles: no network in tests, stable pictures; the docs say a real application passes its tile source.
  3. **AreaMap** — areas (municipalities, a tax's zones, service areas) as lines and light fills in their tone: neutral or one of the five categorical chart colours (`--liro-chart-category1…5`, the validated palette); the chosen area neutral (D17: `border.selected` line, `surface.selected` fill; in the list the selected bar). Each area's name on the map where it fits: placed at its polygon's centroid, or — for a ring around a hole — at the point inside farthest from every line (`labelPoint`); measured and shown only when its box stands wholly inside its area, clear of holes and notches (`labelFits`), never cut; the list names every area. **The list beside the map is the keyboard's and the screen reader's way** (a single-choice listbox, arrows / Home / End move and choose, `areaListKeyTarget`; the choice is announced); a click on an area chooses too. An optional `point` (an address) with "Falls in: Zone II" (`areaAt` / `pointInArea`, built on FloorPlan's `pointInPolygon`); the legend in the application's words. Phones: the point and the list first, the map full width under them. States: loading, empty, error.
  4. **LocationCheck** — made for a phone first: the workplace (a circle of metres or a polygon, dashed in `text.secondary`, 3:1), the person's position (a dot) with its accuracy circle, and **the state in words and tone as the application decides it**: inside (success), outside (warning), unknown (neutral), denied (warning), with the application's detail; the facts in words (the allowed area, the application's distance, the accuracy through `format.number`, the time through `format.time`); "Use my location"; the check-in (or any action needing the place) unavailable with the application's reason (UnavailableAction). `distanceMeters` (haversine, IUGG mean radius) and `withinRadius` are helpers for applications; the verdict is never the component's.
  5. **Accessibility.** The map is one Tab stop, a region named "Map: …", described by its keys: the arrow keys pan and plus / minus zoom (Leaflet's keyboard handler); the buttons zoom and fit. Nothing needs dragging. Every shape is also in words (the list, the facts). Labels on the map are hidden from assistive technology (the list says them).
  6. **Right to left:** a map is **never mirrored** (`dir="ltr"` on it: east stays right; coordinates are never flipped); the list, the facts, the buttons and the legend follow the page.
  - Messages (new, at the end of `messages.ts` under "P5.41 map"): `map.keys`, `map.zoomIn`, `map.zoomOut`, `map.showAll`, `map.name`, `map.areas`, `map.legend`, `map.fallsIn`, `map.fallsInNone`, `map.chosen`, `map.empty`, `map.loading`, `map.error`, `map.retry`, `location.inside`, `location.outside`, `location.unknown`, `location.denied`, `location.locate`, `location.locating`, `location.accuracy`, `location.takenAt`, `location.allowed`, `location.position`.
- **2026-10-14 — Maps in charts: RegionChart (P5.41, owner: "maps in charts — for companies working in several countries, or that want to see their regions").** A choropleth with an optional point or bubble per place, exported from `@veljaos/ui/map` (`RegionChart`, `RegionChartArea`, `RegionChartPlace`), drawn on the same Leaflet SVG map without tiles, and built as a chart of `@veljaos/ui/charts`' kind:
  1. **The charts' frame, reused, without Recharts.** RegionChart stands in the charts' `ChartCard` (title and description, the application's `controls`, the 28px "Show as table" button, the legend above the plot — under it on phones —, the loading skeleton, the empty state `chart.noData` and the error with Retry; `defaultView`), and its tooltip is the charts' `ChartTooltipContent`. That body, the swatch and the legend moved unchanged from `primitives/chart.tsx` to a new `primitives/chart-parts.tsx` (re-exported by `chart.tsx`), so the map subpath imports them without Recharts; `ChartCard` gained a `block` skeleton (one block, for a map). `dist/map.js` imports Leaflet and no Recharts (checked).
  2. **Values are decimal strings (D4).** The step an area falls in is found by comparing its value with the application's `breaks` exactly, as strings (`regionStep`, on QuantityStepper's `compareDecimal`): n breaks make n + 1 steps, at most five (`REGION_MAX_STEPS`); a value at a break belongs to the step above. Only a bubble's size turns a value into a number (`plotValue`, `bubbleRadius`: area by value, 4–22px). Nothing is added, rounded or ranked; the tooltip, the table and the legend write values through `format` (`money` with `currency`, or `number`).
  3. **One meaning in shades.** The ramp is the charts' main blue (`--liro-chart-main`, default) or neutral (`--liro-chart-other`, `ramp="neutral"`), laid on the card at 25% up to 100% (`rampShade`; the map's background is the card's surface for a chart, so the legend's swatches and the areas match; on a dark card the lowest step is the darkest). `color-mix()` was tried first and is refused by `liro/no-raw-colors` (its exceptions live in the protected `eslint.config.mjs`), so the shade is the token at an opacity. Never a rainbow or a status colour.
  4. **No data is hatched and says "No data", never 0** (`map.noData`): a missing or unreadable value has no step; its area is filled with an SVG pattern of `surface.sunken` with `border.strong` lines, its legend entry the same hatch; the tooltip and the table say "No data". With no value at all the card shows the charts' empty state.
  5. **The legend** writes each step's range through `format` in the provider's words — `map.stepBelow` ("Under 1,000,000 EUR"), `map.stepBetween` ("1,000,000 EUR to under 2,000,000 EUR"), `map.stepFrom` ("10,000,000 EUR and more") — or the application's words (`stepLabels`); then "No data" when an area has none, and the places' name (`placeLabel`).
  6. **Keyboard, as the other charts:** the map is one Tab stop, described by `map.regionKeys`; Leaflet's own keys are off for a chart; the arrows move between the areas in reading order — rows north to south (label points within a sixth of the latitude span share a row), each from the page's start; ArrowDown and the arrow toward the line's end go forward (`regionReadingOrder`, `regionKeyTarget`) — show the tooltip at the area's label point, draw a strong `text.primary` line round it, pan it into view when the map was zoomed, and announce "Serbia: 18,640,000 EUR" (`map.areaValue`, a polite status). Enter or Space chooses (`onSelect`), a click too; Escape hides the tooltip. The pointer shows the tooltip over an area. "Show as table" is the screen reader's way: the areas with their values and details, and the places in a second table.
  7. **The chosen area keeps its fill** (its value) and takes the neutral selected line (D17: `border.selected`, 3px).
  8. **Never mirrored**: the map is `dir="ltr"`; the card, the legend, the tooltip's text and the tables follow the page; only the keys' reading order follows the direction.
  9. **Geometry is the application's** (GeoJSON). The stories read Natural Earth's 1:110m countries from **`world-atlas` 2.0.2 (ISC; the data public domain) through `topojson-client` 3.1.0 (ISC), development dependencies of `@veljaos/ui` only** (`map/map-story-world.ts`, not exported; nothing ships in a package). Serbia's okrugs are not in world-atlas: the examples draw five simple regions by hand, labelled "Illustrative".
  - **For the owner to confirm:** Natural Earth draws Kosovo as a polygon of its own. The stories merge it into Serbia's area (`topojson.merge`), so the map has no gap and no separate shape; the geometry of a real application is its own.
  - Messages (new, after LocationCheck's): `map.regionKeys`, `map.noData`, `map.stepBelow`, `map.stepBetween`, `map.stepFrom`, `map.areaValue`.
- **2026-10-14 — Pictures of a map (P5.41).** The stories wait for `data-ready` (`mapReady` in `map-story-data.ts`, up to 10s), set once Leaflet has fitted the view and the labels are measured. No tiles, SVG only, no animation; an untouched map fits its bounds again when its box changes size.
- **2026-10-14 — Example screens of P5.41.** **Group AE** (`screens-AE.tsx`, `data-AE.ts`): Kvadrat Gradnja d.o.o.'s "Property tax zones" (`/finance/property-tax/zones`) — **"Illustrative zones of Novi Sad"**, four hand-drawn rings near 45.25° N, 19.84° E (Zone II and III with the one inside as a hole, Zone IV in two parts across the river), never the city's boundaries and labelled so; the company's three properties (head office in Zone I, Site Liman 4 in Zone II, the warehouse in Zone III; the application decides with `areaAt`), chosen with radios, shown as the map's point. **Group AF** (`screens-AF.tsx`): Nikola Marković checks in at Site Liman 4 from the employee portal (group Y's PortalShell, now exported from `screens-Y.tsx`): inside 150 m of the gate (58 m, accurate to 18 m), outside on the tram (about 800 m), unknown and location off, each with the check-in unavailable and its reason; "Use my location" answers after a moment. **Group AG** (`screens-AG.tsx`, `data-AG.ts`; P5.41 maps in charts): Kvadrat Gradnja group's **"Group revenue by country"** (`/reports/group-revenue`, January–September 2026, EUR): Serbia, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Montenegro, North Macedonia, Croatia, Slovenia and Hungary in five steps, **Albania hatched with "No data"** (its new branch reports from 2027), the subsidiaries' seats as points; and **"Sales by region"** (`/reports/sales-by-region`, RSD): five hand-drawn **illustrative** regions of Serbia (North, Capital, West, East, South; an Alert says they are not the administrative boundaries), the sales offices as bubbles by their own sales. Choosing an area shows its figures in a card under the chart. Every figure is invented.
