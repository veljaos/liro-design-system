# P5.85–P5.88 notes (helper "cases"): alerts, cases, the report to the authority, the overview

Group E, second part, on top of the kyc helper's P5.82–P5.84 (`docs/notes/P5.82-kyc.md`). Examples
only: every screen is built from the library as it is. **No new component, no new prop, no new
message key of its own, no dependency.** The one library piece used that is not yet on this branch
is DefinedForm (P5.66, branch `bp/P5.62-users-documents`): see "Temporary copies" below.

## 1. Decision entries (for `docs/decisions.md`)

- **2026-10-21 — Monitoring alerts are a worklist, decided one by one (P5.85).** The alert queue
  is WorklistPage: each row names the rule that raised the alert, the client with its risk in
  force (a StatusBadge), the transactions' total as the application gives it (MoneyText), the
  alert's age in the application's words and the assignee; the detail holds the rule and what it
  watches (the application's threshold, in its words), the transactions that triggered it with the
  application's marks ("New counterparty", "Cash", "Just below the threshold") and the total it
  gives (`totals` of the DataTable — never summed), the client's profile (risk, file state, PEP,
  expected activity, source of funds, screening) and the alert's history (HistoryList). "Close…"
  asks for the reason the application requires (ReasonConfirmDialog, neutral; several alerts at
  once from the BulkActionBar with one reason); "Escalate to a case…" is a Dialog with the case to
  go to (the client's open cases, or "Open a new case"), the application's reasons and details,
  caution family — then the case opens. An unassigned alert offers "Assign to me". A rule's
  figures, thresholds and ages are the application's: the screens count rows, never amounts.
- **2026-10-21 — A case is a DocumentPage (P5.86).** The client is the counterparty; the key
  figures are the risk in force, when it was opened, who investigates and the application's total
  of the transactions under review; the lines are the transactions of the linked alerts; the
  sections are the timeline (StatusTimeline with the next step: who approves next, the report to
  prepare), the linked alerts, screening matches and report (RelatedDocuments), the documents
  (AttachmentList), the tasks (a DataTable: task, owner, state in words with the reason a blocked
  one waits) and the decision; the side panels are the notes (ActivityList with a note field),
  the client and the history. The case's own states are Under investigation → Decision proposed →
  Approved → Closed, no suspicion / Reported; the lifecycle bar and the timeline name the last
  step by the outcome. Every case page starts with the `ConfidentialNote` as DocumentPage's
  `banner`.
- **2026-10-21 — Four eyes as a SignerList (P5.86).** A decision is proposed by one person
  ("Propose decision" → "No suspicion…" / "Report to the authority…", each a ReasonConfirmDialog
  with the application's reasons and the reasoning as details) and approved by another: the
  proposer and the approver are a SignerList — the proposer "signed" at the proposal's time, the
  approver waiting, the current user's row with `signLabel="Approve"` and Decline (with the
  application's reasons; the case goes back to the investigation and keeps the declined proposal
  in its history). The proposer cannot approve: the approver is the other of the compliance
  officer and the deputy, and the rule is written beside the list ("Four eyes" Alert, neutral).
  Approving "no suspicion" closes the case; approving a report opens the report as a draft
  (P5.87) and the case waits as Approved until the report is sent, then it is Reported.
- **2026-10-21 — The report is a DefinedForm from the authority's definitions (P5.87).** The
  suspicious transaction report is the illustrative authority's form kept by the application as
  field definitions (sections, fields, options, indicator codes, conditions — a legal person has a
  tax number, a natural person a date of birth; "Stopped until" shows when the funds were
  stopped), drawn by DefinedForm with the values the application filled in from the firm's record
  and the case. The page is PageHeader + ConfidentialNote + the form, with the progress
  (StatusTimeline: Draft → In review → Approved → Submitted → Acknowledged, the next step and its
  action), who prepared and who reviews (never the same person), and the history beside it (a
  320px column from `lg`; on a phone everything in one column, the actions in the bottom bar).
  "Send for review" runs the form's `check()`: the missing values are said under their fields in
  the library's words, and a toast only reports that the report is not complete. The reviewer
  approves or sends it back with a reason; "Submit to the authority…" confirms what happens
  (caution ConfirmDialog). After that the form is read-only and "What was sent" records the
  instant, who sent it, the channel, the reference, the acknowledgement and the files kept — the
  files are the application's, the library renders no document.
- **2026-10-21 — The overview counts nothing (P5.88).** The AML overview is a DashboardPage: the
  figures over all 214 clients are the application's (`OVERVIEW_FIGURES`); only the open alerts
  and cases of the session are counted as rows. The clients by risk are a DonutChart whose slices
  carry the states' tones (danger, warning, success) and are named in the legend and the centre;
  the reviews due by month a BarChart with "Overdue" highlighted; the ageing a stacked BarChart of
  alerts and cases; alerts raised and closed two series. The compliance officer and deputy stand
  in a SectionCard with the work waiting; the year's obligations are a SetupChecklist (done / to
  do / blocked with the reason). The obligations page (DetailPage, section bar) holds the officer
  and deputy with their appointments and when they were reported to the authority, the staff's
  training records (a DataTable; the state — Done / Due / Overdue — is the application's word), the
  internal procedures as an AttachmentList with their adoption and review dates (a flag "Review
  due" from the application), the retention rules as a KeyValueList and the records whose period
  ends as a table, and the audit trail as a HistoryList paged by the application ("Show more").
- **2026-10-21 — Where the confidentiality note stands (P5.85–P5.88).** The kyc helper's
  `ConfidentialNote` (a warning Alert titled "Confidential", the words the application's) opens
  every case page (DocumentPage `banner`), every report page (under the header) and the detail of
  an alert that is part of a case. An open alert carries no such note: it is not yet a suspicion;
  its dialogs say "The client is not told."
- **2026-10-21 — The monitored company (P5.85).** The transactions monitored are the ones the
  accounting firm books and handles for its clients: Adria Commodities d.o.o. (the invented metals
  wholesaler of data-aml) is the trading company whose payments abroad and round advances raise
  the alerts and the case; Kvadrat Gradnja d.o.o.'s alert is its receipt from Stanogradnja Plus
  d.o.o. for order NAR-2026-0024 (data-AV). No second shell or module was built: one AML module of
  Knjigovodstvo Jelić monitors its clients' transactions, as an obligated accounting firm does.

## New components and why

None. Considered and not needed:

- An "alert queue" component: WorklistPage with KeyFigures, FilterBar and BulkActionBar is the
  queue; the detail is KeyValueList, DataTable and HistoryList.
- A "case" template: DocumentPage carries a case as it carries a document — the client is the
  counterparty, the transactions are the lines, the decision and the tasks are sections, the notes
  and the history are panels.
- A "four-eyes approval" component: SignerList with `signLabel="Approve"` and its decline dialog
  is the pattern; the rule is a sentence from the application.
- A "tasks" component: a DataTable of title, owner and state in words (SetupChecklist would put a
  second heading inside a DocumentPage section; on the overview it is used as the year's
  obligations, where its heading is the card's).
- An "escalate" dialog component: Dialog with two RadioGroupFields and a TextAreaField is one
  decision; ReasonConfirmDialog has no room for the case to go to.
- A "submission record": a SectionCard with KeyValueList and AttachmentList.
