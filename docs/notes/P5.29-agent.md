## 1. Decisions (for docs/decisions.md, "Liro patterns (Phase 5 part 2)")

- **2026-10-14 — The helpdesk is built from the library (P5.30 a–c).** The agent's inbox is
  ListPage + FilterBar + DataTable (views My open, Unassigned, Overdue, All with counts; "Assign…"
  and "Change status…" on the BulkActionBar, each asking in one Dialog whose title is a question
  with the count, D22); the ticket page is DetailPage (one "Conversation" section: MessageList and
  ReplyComposer) with SidePanels in the 300px side column (Properties changed in place with
  SelectFields, Service level, Contact, Other tickets as RelatedDocuments, Activity as
  ActivityList); the dashboard is DashboardPage with six StatCards, two BarCharts, a LineChart, a
  DonutChart and the overdue tickets in a card. Phones: the conversation full width, the panels in
  an end Drawer opened by "Details" (with the ticket as `subject`), the composer under the
  conversation. Three new pieces only, each generic: SlaTimer, ReplyComposer and the `internal`
  message.
- **2026-10-14 — SlaTimer: a deadline in words against the application's `now` (P5.30 a).** A due
  instant, the application's `now` (never the device's clock, as the NowMarker, D25), optional
  `paused`, `completedAt` and `warnWithin`: "Due in 2 h 15 min" (text.secondary, a clock), the
  same in the warning tone within `warnWithin` minutes, "Overdue by 40 min" in the danger tone with
  an alert icon and medium weight, "Paused" (a pause icon), "Met" (success) or "Missed by 25 min"
  (danger). The words always carry the state, never the colour alone. Minutes are whole: the time
  left is rounded up (30 seconds is "1 min"), the time late rounded down (right at the deadline:
  "Overdue"); under an hour "40 min", under a day "2 h 15 min", from a day "1 d 3 h" (minutes left
  out). `label` names the deadline before its words (two in a table cell); `showDue` writes the due
  instant under them. The arithmetic is pure and tested (`sla-logic.ts`: `slaReading`,
  `durationParts`, `slaUrgency` for the application's "most urgent first" order). It does not
  tick: the application passes a new `now`.
- **2026-10-14 — Internal notes in a conversation (P5.30 b).** `ThreadMessage.internal` /
  `MessageBubble internal`: the message stays on its author's side, on a new Bubble surface `note`
  — surface.sunken with a **dashed border.strong** line — and starts with a visible "Internal"
  mark (a lock and the word, `messages['message.internal']`), read first by assistive technology.
  A note differs from a reply by its line and its word, never by colour; no warning tone (a note
  is not a problem).
- **2026-10-14 — ReplyComposer (P5.30 b), in `@veljaos/ui/rich-text`.** RichTextField with two
  modes (`modes`, default reply and note; two pressed buttons in a group "Message type", the
  neutral selection; one mode shows no switch); the note's text box on surface.sunken with "Only
  your team sees internal notes." beside the switch; "@" mentions only in the note mode (a reply
  leaves the team). Tools under the text: the paperclip (`onAttach`, `accept`; dropping files on
  the composer is the shortcut; the files are the application's, shown in `attachments` — an
  AttachmentList), `onInsertLink` (the application opens its own search, e.g. a knowledge base in
  LookupDialog, and resolves `{ text, href }`; inserted at the caret, http(s) and mailto only),
  saved replies (`savedReplies`, grouped, searched by typing from 11 as SelectField, D20; inserted
  at the caret — a one-paragraph reply joins the line). Send (the primary family, light weight) or
  Ctrl/Cmd+Enter; a promise keeps the text with "Sending…" and empties it when it resolves. Labels
  from messages or `replyLabel` / `noteLabel` / `noteHint` / `insertLinkLabel`. No helpdesk word
  in the component.
- **2026-10-14 — RichTextField can be reached from outside (P5.30 b).** `ref` gives a handle:
  `insert(content)` at the caret where it was before a menu or dialog took the focus (a document,
  nodes or text; `insertable` decides), `focus()` and `clear()`. `surface` 'sunken' draws the text
  box on surface.sunken.
- **2026-10-14 — A list worked from the keyboard (P5.30 a).** DataTable's pressable rows (and
  phone cards) move the focus with ArrowDown / ArrowUp, Home and End (`rowFocusTarget`, pure and
  tested; `focusNeighbourRow` in data-table-card.tsx), besides Tab; Space presses and Enter opens
  as before. Every list with `onRowClick` gains it.
- **2026-10-14 — Ticket statuses and their tones are the application's (P5.30 examples).** Open
  warning (waits on the team), Replied info (on its way to the customer), Paused neutral, Resolved
  success, Closed neutral: `TICKET_TONES` in `data-S.ts`, not in example-shell's TONES, so the
  portal helper's map cannot collide with it (see "For the lead").
