# P5.76 Property management — notes for the lead (helper "property", examples group BQ)

## Decision entries

- **2026-10-16 — Property management is assembled, nothing new (P5.76).** Kvadrat Nekretnine
  d.o.o. (the Kvadrat group's company for the sale and rental of flats, data-AJ, PIB 111985302)
  lets the units of two buildings Kvadrat Gradnja d.o.o. built — Residential building Liman 2
  (2021) and Residential building Grbavica 7 (2023), both illustrative — and its property manager
  Vesna Tomić works them on 14.10.2026. Units are RoomRack by building and floor (the occupancy
  states Let / Vacant / Under repair in words, the rent's state Rent paid / Rent overdue as the
  rack's second state, the tenant and the lease's term on each tile, notes such as "Lease ends
  31.12.2026"), a unit opens in a Drawer beside the rack (as the hotel's rooms); the same units as
  a list (ListPage, FilterBar, DataTable). Tenants and leases are ListPages and DetailPages; the
  tenant's national ID is MaskedValue (P5.14: the reason asked, access logged); the lease's
  renewal and expiry reminders are the application's words in an Alert (its action neutral, D23),
  Renew… is a Dialog with the lease as `subject` (D22) that sends an annex for signature; the
  lease document opens on a page of its own in DocumentFrame (the stories' stand-in viewer).
  Monthly rent invoicing is PeriodicRunPage (lock, steps Prepare → Calculate → Review → Issue and
  send, checks in words, the preview with every invoice and the totals, Rerun with a reason); one
  invoice opens as it is printed (PrintDocument in a wide Dialog, as the statements of group W).
  Maintenance requests are a ListPage of states and who handles each, a request opening in a
  Drawer (the tenant's photos as AttachmentList, the progress as StatusTimeline, "Handled by" as
  a required SelectField, the work order as a link). The tenants' portal is PortalShell (Home,
  Requests, Invoices) with "Report a problem" (what, where, description, photos through
  FileDropzone `camera`, may we enter, phone). Every rent, charge, VAT amount, total, count, date
  and reminder is the application's (data-BQ.ts); the screens add and count nothing (D4); a
  request reported in the portal appears in the manager's list (state above the screens).
- **2026-10-16 — RoomRack for units through the application's messages (P5.76).** RoomRack's
  hidden keyboard instructions and empty state say "rooms"; for units the example passes the
  application's own `rack.instructions`, `rack.empty` and `rack.emptyDescription` through its
  LiroProvider `messages` (D2) and uses the existing `searchLabel`, `occupancyLabel` and
  `housekeepingLabel` props ("Unit", "Rent"). No new prop.
- **2026-10-16 — The field-service work order is linked by its route (P5.76).** A maintenance
  request's work order (SN-2026-0087 for the leaking sink, SN-2026-0081, SN-2026-0079,
  SN-2026-0074, SN-2026-0070, SN-2026-0019) links to `/service/work-orders/<number>`, the route
  agreed for group C's field service (P5.74, screens-BO), which is not in this branch: until BO
  is merged the link opens the examples' "not found" page. The numbers are mine; BO may use them
  or the lead changes them in `data-BQ.ts` (`workOrderRoute`, `REQUESTS[].workOrder`).
