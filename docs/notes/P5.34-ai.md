# Notes for the lead: P5.34, P5.38, P5.39 (helper "ai")

Branch `p7/ai`. Examples X (Liro Assistant), AB (e-mail templates), AC (blog).

## 1. Decisions (for `docs/decisions.md`)

### The Liro Assistant (P5.34)

- **2026-10-14 — The assistant is data, never a model (P5.34).** Nothing in the library or the examples calls a model. An answer is the application's data: a list of blocks (`AnswerBlock`: `heading`, `text`, `list`, `table`, `chart`, `sources`) and a state (`AnswerState`: thinking, streaming, done, stopped, error, noAccess). "Streaming" is a state the application renders — the text it has so far, "Writing…" and Stop; nothing in the library runs a timer. The examples choose a fixed answer object by its question (`data-X.ts`, `answerKeyOf`).
- **2026-10-14 — Four generic pieces and a page (P5.34).** `AssistantAnswer` (one answer), `ProposedAction` (an action the assistant proposes), `AssistantComposer` (the question box), `AssistantPanel` (the side panel) and the template `AskPage`. No domain word inside: "invoice", "post", "salaries" are the application's words. Logic in `assistant-logic.ts` (unit-tested: `answerBusy`, `shownBlocks`, `followUpsShown`, `canReport`, `questionReady`, `questionKey`, `actionOffers`, `actionSettled`, `answerSources`).
- **2026-10-14 — Labelled AI-generated; a wrong answer is reported (P5.34).** Over every answer the assistant's name and AgentMark with the words "AI-generated" (`messages['assistant.generated']`; AgentMark gained an optional `label`, default still "Agent"). Neutral, never blue (D17). Under a complete or stopped answer "Report a wrong answer" (a small neutral text button with a flag) opens ReasonConfirmDialog — titled by the action, the question as its subject (D22), the application's reasons or a required text; `onReport` receives the answer; then "Thank you. Your report was sent." (`reported` controls it).
- **2026-10-14 — An answer's blocks (P5.34).** Paragraphs and headings take their direction from their content (D15). A **table** is a real table (caption, column headers, numbers at the end in tabular digits, a total row the application computed) on the "Ask" page; in the side panel (`size="panel"`) and on phones each row is a small card — the first cell as its title, the others as label / value lines — so nothing scrolls sideways (D19). A row's `href` links its first cell to the record it came from. A **chart** is a slot (`kind: 'chart'`, a ReactNode): the application passes one from `@veljaos/ui/charts`, so the main entry never loads Recharts. **Sources** are a numbered list of links with a detail line, under "Sources".
- **2026-10-14 — The answer's states (P5.34).** Thinking: a spinner and "Thinking…" (polite status), no blocks. Streaming: the blocks so far, "Writing…" and Stop (neutral). Stopped: the blocks written, "Stopped. The answer may be incomplete." and Try again. Error: the application's words in a danger Alert with Try again as a neutral secondary button (D23). No access: the application's words ("You do not have permission to see salaries.") in a **neutral** Alert with a lock — a permission is not a failure. While thinking or streaming the answer is `aria-busy`, so assistive technology reads it once it is written. Follow-ups (the application's questions as buttons) only under a complete answer.
- **2026-10-14 — A proposed action always waits (P5.34).** `ProposedAction` is a raised card with a border (never a tone: a proposal is not a status): "Proposed action" (Sparkles, xs text.secondary), the title, a line, the facts (KeyValueList), "Nothing is done until you confirm.", then Dismiss and Confirm (the main action last, D13). Only Confirm runs it, through the application's `onConfirm`; a promise shows "Working…" with both buttons waiting; then a polite status with the application's outcome ("Posted on 14.10.2026. as entry 2026-0884." with a link), or "Dismissed. Nothing was done."; `failed` shows the application's words in a danger Alert and Confirm can be pressed again. `unavailableReason` disables Confirm and says why in words.
- **2026-10-14 — The side panel on every page (P5.34).** `AssistantPanel` is a sheet at the end (440px, full height; the whole screen on a phone, D19), opened from the application's button in AppShell's `agent` slot (`trigger`; the examples use a neutral IconButton with Sparkles, "Liro Assistant"). Header: the assistant's name, "New conversation" (28px pencil) and "Open as a page" (a link to the application's AskPage), Close. The conversation is a log: each question on surface.sunken at the end side ("You asked" for assistive technology), each answer an AssistantAnswer; when it opens or a turn is added, the last question is scrolled to the top of the view. Before the first question: the application's intro and suggested questions. The question box stays at the bottom with the application's note ("Answers use only the data you may see. Check them before you act."). It is modal (a Drawer's rules): the page is there again when it closes.
- **2026-10-14 — The question box (P5.34).** `AssistantComposer`: one box with the ask button (ArrowUp, primary family; filled once there is a question, named "Ask") inside at its end. Enter asks, Shift+Enter starts a new line, Enter while an input method composes a word types (`questionKey`). It grows with the text up to 12 lines (`field-sizing: content`). `size="page"`: 16px text, 3 lines, a soft shadow and "Enter asks, Shift+Enter starts a new line" under it. `busy` keeps the ask button waiting while an answer is written (Stop is the answer's). Disabled with a visible reason.
- **2026-10-14 — The "Ask" page has nothing else on it (P5.34).** `AskPage`: before a question one centred 42rem column — the application's title as the h1, its line, the large box, the suggested and recent questions (two columns on a desktop, one on a phone). With a question: a 44rem reading column — "New question", the question as the h1, the answer (`size="page"`: h2 headings, tables as tables, charts, sources) and the box for a follow-up ("Ask a follow-up"). Inside the application's shell; no side panels, figures or lists.
- **2026-10-14 — The examples (P5.34, group X).** Kvadrat Gradnja d.o.o. on 14.10.2026 at 09:40; Milica Petrović asks beside Receivables (seven open invoices, 2.690.700,00 RSD). "Which invoices are overdue more than 30 days?": three (F-2026-0341, -0352, -0358; 512.400,00 RSD), each linked to its record page, sources Receivables and bank statement 188. "How did revenue change this quarter?": 14.860.000,00 RSD, 12,4% over Q3 2025, a BarChart (2026 against 2025). "Post the Beton Mix invoice": invoice 2026-1187 (245.000,00 + VAT 20% 49.000,00 = 294.000,00 RSD, due 11.11.2026) as a ProposedAction; Confirm gives entry 2026-0884. "What is the average gross salary on the sites?": no access. The sums are checked when the examples load (`data-X.ts`, as dataset-checks.ts). Invoice numbers chosen not to collide with the other datasets.

### E-mail and notification templates (P5.38)

- **2026-10-14 — EmailPreview: a message as its reader sees it (P5.38).** New `EmailPreview`: From, To and Subject over a thin rule, the sender's logo at the start, the body, the footer in small grey text under a thin rule — as simple as the printouts (D27): black and grey, no boxes, frames or shaded bands, the brand colour only in links. Two segmented switches over it: Width (Desktop 600px / Phone 360px, never wider than the room) and Mail client (Light / Dark); the message resolves its tokens in the chosen scheme (`data-liro-theme`), as a dark mail client inverts a message whatever the page's theme. Controlled or not; `controls={false}` hides the switches; `lang` marks a variant in another language (D15).
- **2026-10-14 — Merge fields filled for a preview (P5.38).** `@veljaos/ui/rich-text` gained `fillMergeFields(doc, values)` (each merge field with a value becomes its text, marks kept; one without a value stays a pill, so the preview shows what is missing; no empty text node left), `fillMergeText(text, values)` for a plain subject with `{{key}}`, and `mergeTextKeys(text)`. Pure and tested (`merge-fill.test.ts`). The application fills real messages on its server.
- **2026-10-14 — The template editor is built from the library (P5.38, group AB).** ListPage + DataTable for the list (template, when it is sent, its languages "en · sr-Latn · sr-Cyrl", last change, Active / Draft). One template: PageHeader (Versions, Send test, Save, the main last), the languages as Tabs (English, Srpski (latinica), Српски (ћирилица) — each label with its `lang`; never a bare `sr`, D12), and in each tab two SectionCards side by side on a desktop, under each other on a phone: "Message" (Subject TextField with `{{field}}`, Body RichTextField with the merge fields as pills, grouped) and "Preview" (EmailPreview with Gradska toplana Novi Sad's sample: F-2026-0423, 1.284.600,00 RSD due 30.10.2026.). A missing language: EmptyState with "Start from English". Versions in an end Drawer with Restore (ConfirmDialog titled by the action, the template as its subject); "Send test" a Dialog (address, sample data) whose toast says where it went. Dialogs and drawers at page level, outside the Tabs (D16).

### The blog (P5.39)

- **2026-10-14 — Reading pieces, generic (P5.39).** `ArticleList` (articles as cards without frames: the cover in a 16:9 slot, decorative; the category; the title as the card's link — its press area covers the card, the link named by the title alone; three lines of excerpt; "author · date · reading time"; columns of at least 17rem, one on phones; `featured` puts the first across the row with its cover beside the text; loading, empty, error), `NewsletterSignup` (a sunken band: title, line, the address — left to right, autofill — and Subscribe, the consent note; an empty or misshaped address said under the field before anything is sent, `looksLikeEmail`; a promise shows "Subscribing…"; then "Thank you. Check your inbox to confirm."), `ShareLinks` ("Share", "Copy link" with a polite "Link copied", and the application's places to share as neutral link buttons with their words — never a network's colour or logo; external ones open in a new tab) and the template `ArticlePage` (a 44rem reading column: back and the category link, the h1, the lede, the byline with the author's avatar, date, "Updated …" and reading time, the cover, the body, the tags, share, newsletter and the related articles under "Related articles"; a long article's PageContents in a 14rem column at the start, over the body on a phone). Logic in `blog-logic.ts` (tested).
- **2026-10-14 — Covers are drawings in the tokens' colours (P5.39).** No images and no raw colours: the examples' and the stories' covers are inline SVG filled with `var(--liro-…)` meanings (surface.sunken, brand.subtle, a chart category, border.strong), decided by a number, so they follow the theme.
- **2026-10-14 — The blog and its authoring (P5.39, group AC).** The public blog in PortalShell (brand "Liro Blog", no participant): the list with the lead story, the search (a `role="search"` form; results in SearchResultList with the matches marked), the categories as links with the current one the neutral selection (`aria-current="page"`), popular tags, the newsletter. The article: ArticlePage with RichText `headingIds`, PageContents (from four headings), ShareLinks (E-mail, LinkedIn), NewsletterSignup, related articles. Authoring for the Liro team (Jelena Stanković): ListPage of posts (All, Drafts, Scheduled, Published), and the editor on RecordFormPage — the text (title, excerpt, RichTextField), the cover (a drawing to choose, or FileDropzone for an image), where it appears (address `slug` left to right with the full URL under it, category, tags, author), search engines (title and description with their lengths, and the result as SearchResultList shows it). Draft → Schedule… (a Dialog: date and time, Belgrade) or Publish (ConfirmDialog: "It appears on the blog now, for everyone, and in the next newsletter."); Scheduled → Unschedule / Publish now; Published → Unpublish / Save changes. A post cannot go out without a title, an address in lowercase letters, digits and hyphens, a search description and its text: each said under its field. Preview shows the article as readers will see it under an info Banner "Preview" with "Back to editing". A new status tone: Published (success).
