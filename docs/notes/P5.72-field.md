# P5.74 and P5.75 — field service and maintenance, fleet (helper "field")

## Decisions (for docs/decisions.md)

- **2026-10-16 — Field service and maintenance is assembled, not a component (P5.74).**
  Kvadrat Gradnja d.o.o.'s machines are group AP's fixed assets (the mini excavator OS-0142, the
  tower crane OS-0118, the generator OS-0146, the mixer, the bender, the total station, the
  scaffolding); the dispatcher is Marko Đorđević (procurement and warehouse manager, cost centre
  400 Machinery pool, group AJ); the work is done by the service partners' technicians under their
  service contracts — Saša Vukadinović of Mehanizacija Bačka d.o.o. (who sold the excavator) — so
  group AJ's forty people stay as they are; spare parts are issued from the Central warehouse
  (group AY). The work orders are ListPage + FilterBar + DataTable with "Assign…" in the row's
  menu (Dialog). The technician's work order is DetailPage in the phone layout: the fault and the
  service due (KeyValueList), the machine with the hour meter (NumberField), the checklist
  (CheckboxFields, "5 of 9 done" as the section's description, read-only once signed), photos
  (FileDropzone `camera` + AttachmentList), parts (QuantityStepper per issued part, "Add a part"
  ComboboxField; read-only as a DataTable with Taken / Used), time (DataTable, "Stop work"), what
  was done (TextAreaField), signatures (SignerList). Offline-first as group I: AppShell's
  `offlineIndicator` (OfflineIndicator with what waits on the phone, "Try now") and ConnectionState
  in the bottom bar above "Finish and sign" (UnavailableAction with the reason until the checklist
  is done). The work order is numbered SN-2026-01xx ("Work order" RN- is production's, group BA).
- **2026-10-16 — The customer's signature on the technician's phone is SignerList, no pad
  (P5.74).** "Finish and sign" signs for the technician (his account); the phone is then handed
  to the site manager: the application marks her as the person at the device (`current`), so her
  row reads "(you)" and has Sign and Decline (with a reason, which sends the order back to the
  technician as a warning Alert). Her part comes first on that screen (an Alert "please check the
  work and sign", what was done, then the rest). A drawn signature pad, when an application needs
  one, is the application's (as the signing itself is the Core's, P5.21); no new prop was needed.
- **2026-10-16 — The preventive maintenance plan is a DataTable by machine and task (P5.74).**
  The interval, last done and next due (a date, or a reading "At 1.250 h") and the state as a
  badge (Overdue danger, Due soon warning, Planned neutral) with the application's words under it
  ("12 h overdue (1.262 h on 14.10.2026.)"); the open work order as a link, or "Create work order"
  (neutral secondary button in the cell) opening a Dialog (technician, day); the application
  numbers it (SN-2026-0146) and it appears in the work orders. A table rather than CalendarView:
  half of the tasks fall due by operating hours, which have no day.
- **2026-10-16 — Fleet is assembled (P5.75).** Vehicles (ListPage: plate and model, driver,
  mileage, the registration's expiry and the next service each as the date or reading with the
  application's words in its tone's text colour, the state), one vehicle (DetailPage: key figures
  — mileage, the application's consumption, fuel this year, next service in danger when overdue —,
  the vehicle and how it is held — operating lease, or fixed asset OS-0133 on finance lease
  LZ-2024-012 —, registration and insurance with expiry, its reminders, services with invoices,
  fuel with litres, cost, mileage and the application's l/100 km, "Add fuel entry" Dialog), the
  reminders (ListPage: registration, insurance, service, tyres; Overdue / Due soon / Planned /
  Done with words; "Mark as done…" in the row's menu). Only the pickup is a fixed asset; the other
  cars are on operating leases (so group AP's register stays fourteen assets); the van NS-123-AB is
  the one the cash book fuels (CP-2026-0210, CP-2026-0214, linked); the Ducato sold on 30.09.2026
  is in "Sold and returned"; the rented truck NS-377-ZK (group AQ) has no reminders.
  "Due" words come from data, not DueDate, because the application words a reading ("Overdue by
  2.880 km") the same way as a date.
