## 1. Decision entries (for docs/decisions.md)

- **2026-10-16 — Scheduled reports are built from the library (P5.80, group BU).** Nothing new was
  needed. The schedules are ListPage + DataTable (views All / Active / Paused / Last run failed
  with counts): the report and its kind, the schedule in the application's words ("Every Monday
  at 07:00"), who gets it ("Nenad Kovačević", "2 people, 1 outside"), the format, the next run
  (the application's instant through `format.dateTime`, "Paused" in words while paused), the last
  run's state as a badge with its time, and a SwitchField per row ("Sending: <name>", the label
  for assistive technology) that pauses or resumes it; the row's menu has Edit and Pause /
  Resume. One schedule is DetailPage: Pause / Resume and Send now (neutral), Edit (the main one,
  last); key figures next run, last run, its state, recipients; sections Report (the report and
  each parameter's chosen value — ReportPage's parameters —, the format, what happens when there
  is nothing to send), Schedule (repeats, the time zone "Europe/Belgrade (CEST, UTC+02:00)", the
  application's next three runs, who paused it and why, who created it), Recipients (people with
  their role and address, outside addresses with "Outside the company · Knjigovodstvo Jelić"),
  Last runs (DataTable: when, Sent / Failed / Skipped, "1 of 1" delivered, the file and its size,
  the application's note; Download in the row's menu) and "What recipients get" (EmailPreview
  with the sample values filled in, D27). A failed last run stands first as a danger Alert with
  the application's message and neutral actions Send now and Edit (D23). **Send now** asks in a
  ConfirmDialog titled by the action with the schedule as its subject (D22) and says the schedule
  does not change; the run joins the list. A new or edited schedule is RecordFormPage: Report
  (name, the report, its parameters as SelectFields that reset to the report's defaults when the
  report changes), Schedule (Repeats from the application's list — every day / week / month /
  quarter —, On: a weekday whose name comes from the provider's locale, or a day 1–28 / the last
  day from the application's list; At: the application's half hours, searched from 11 options;
  the time zone and the next run read-only — the next run is the application's, "The application
  plans it when you save" after a change), Recipients (MultiSelectField of the company's people;
  MultiSelectField of outside addresses used before; "Add an outside address" with the
  application's check under the field and "Add address"), Delivery (format PDF / Excel / CSV and
  "When there is nothing to send" as RadioGroupFields with descriptions), E-mail (subject with
  `{{date}}` / `{{period}}`, message) and the Preview. **No date of a run is computed by the
  library or by the screens**: every next run and every state is data.
- **2026-10-16 — DashboardGrid: a dashboard the user arranges (P5.81).** New, because nothing
  arranged cards of different widths: Launchpad's editing moves and hides module cards of one
  size and links; DashboardPage lays out a fixed set (stats, then two columns); KanbanBoard moves
  cards between stages. `DashboardGrid` takes the application's `cards` (id, title, size, the
  sizes it allows, its content — StatCard, a chart, a SectionCard), the application's `sizes`
  (name and columns), `columns` (default 4) and `editing`, and reports every change with the new
  layout and what changed (`onLayoutChange(layout, { kind: 'move' | 'resize' | 'remove', … })`);
  the application keeps the draft, saves it on its Done, puts it back on Cancel and adds cards
  from its catalogue (`addCard`). Grid: `columns` from 62em, two from 36em, one on phones; a card
  takes its size's columns, at most the grid's (two in the two-column grid, one on phones); the
  browser places the cards in order, never "dense", so the reading order is the visual order;
  heights follow the content. View mode adds nothing around the cards. **Editing:** each card in
  a dashed border.strong frame (radius lg, 8px) with a toolbar over its content — the handle
  (GripVertical 28px, "Move: <title>", the instructions as its description), the title (sm
  semibold), the size button (the size's name and a chevron; a menu of the sizes the card allows
  as radio items with a check), the card's menu (Move earlier, Move later, Move to the start,
  Move to the end) and Remove (X, 28px); the content stays visible and inert. Remove asks
  nothing: the application's Cancel puts the card back. **Moving, three ways (WCAG 2.5.7):** drag
  ("Launchpad dragging is live": pointer events, a mouse after 4px, a touch after a 250ms press,
  the card lifts and follows the pointer, a neutral dashed placeholder of its size where it
  lands, the slot nearest its centre measured when the drag starts — right-to-left needs no
  mirroring —, Escape or a cancelled pointer puts it back); the handle (Space / Enter picks it
  up, the reading direction's forward arrow moves it one place later and the other earlier,
  ArrowUp / ArrowDown to the place of the card above or below as laid out — `gridPlacement`
  reproduces the browser's placement, `dashboardKeyTarget` picks the card sharing the most
  columns —, Home / End, Space / Enter drops, Escape cancels, Tab away puts it back; each step
  announced politely, "Revenue: position 2 of 7."); the card's menu. **Phones:** one column, every
  card full width, no handle and no dragging, no size button (every size is the full width
  there); the menu moves and Remove removes. Loading keeps each card's place with a skeleton;
  without cards the application's `empty` (default EmptyState "No cards on this dashboard.").
  Pure and tested: `moveCardTo`, `moveCardBy`, `resizeCard`, `removeCard`, `addCard`, `layoutOf`,
  `cardSpan`, `allowedSizes`, `gridPlacement`, `dashboardKeyTarget`.
- **2026-10-16 — The director's customizable overview (P5.81, group BV).** DashboardPage with
  DashboardGrid as its one child (`col-span-full`). The header: the layout as a SelectField
  ("My overview · Default", "Month end"), a "Layout" menu (Save as…, Rename…, Set as default,
  Delete…) and Customize; while customizing: Add card, Cancel, Done (the one primary button,
  last) and a subtitle that says how. Add card is LookupDialog over the application's catalogue
  (the cards not yet on the layout, searched by name or kind; a card is added at the end at its
  catalogue size). Save as and Rename are FileNameDialog (the application's check returns "A
  layout with this name exists. Choose another name."); Delete is DeleteConfirmDialog with the
  layout as its subject ("Only the layout is deleted, never the figures"); deleting the default
  makes the next one the default (the application's rule, said in the message). The cards show
  the figures the other examples show: open receivables and overdue more than 30 days (group X),
  the main cash desk against its limit (AM), the budget used and plan against actual by cost
  centre (AR), revenue by month (X), Residential building Liman 4's progress (AA), the overdue
  invoices as a table (X), Liman 4 by cost type (AR).
- **2026-10-16 — Two statuses join the examples' tone map (P5.80).** `Skipped` (a run with
  nothing to send) and `Paused` (a schedule paused on purpose) are neutral: they wait on no one.
  A report run that was sent keeps the map's `Sent` (info), as every sent thing does.

## New components and why (the owner's list)

- **DashboardGrid** (P5.81) — new. Nothing existing arranged cards of different widths: Launchpad
  reorders and hides module links of one size (its editing pattern — move buttons named with the
  card, live pointer dragging, the hidden list — was reused: the same `dragTargetIndex` and the
  same dragging rule); DashboardPage lays out a fixed set; KanbanBoard moves cards between
  columns (its keyboard handle was reused: the same Space / arrows / Escape and announcements).
  A prop on DashboardPage would have had to carry the whole editing mode; a component of its own
  can sit in DashboardPage (as its one child) or in any page.
- **P5.80 scheduled reports** — no new component and no new prop: ListPage, DataTable,
  SwitchField, DetailPage, KeyValueList, Alert, ConfirmDialog, EmailPreview, RecordFormPage,
  FormSection, SelectField, MultiSelectField, TextField, RadioGroupField, TextAreaField.
  Outside addresses are a TextField + "Add address" beside a MultiSelectField whose options the
  application extends; MultiSelectField takes no free text, and a new prop for it was not needed.
