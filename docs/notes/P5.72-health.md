## Decision entries (for `docs/decisions.md`)

- **2026-10-16 — The patient chart is assembled, no new component (P5.72).** Jovana Nikolić's chart
  at Ambulanta Liman of Dom zdravlja Novi Sad (group H's patient, doctors and routes), seen on
  14.10.2026 at 09:40, two hours before the check-up she booked, is a DetailPage: the visits first
  (a DataTable by date, newest first; a press opens the visit in an end Drawer — D14, a read-only
  view with the list still visible — with its finding, the medicines given and the laboratory
  order), the findings, the medicines (current first, then the past ones: dose, directions, from,
  until — "No end date" — prescriber and the application's state), the latest laboratory results
  (value, unit, the laboratory's reference range, the verdict as a StatusBadge in the
  application's words and tone), and SidePanels with the patient, her allergy and the next
  appointment. A timeline component was not needed: HistoryList is a record's change history
  (actors, old → new), ActivityList shows the latest two and StatusTimeline is one process's
  states; a dated DataTable is what an outpatient chart reads like, and it becomes cards on phones.
- **2026-10-16 — Health data in the chart (P5.72).** The diagnosis and the finding's text are
  MaskedValues as in P5.14 and group H's finding: each one asks for its own reason (the clinic's
  list and Other), says "Access is logged", and shows the value only after the application passes
  it (in the findings table one mask per finding; in the visit drawer the diagnosis and the
  finding's text separately). A recorded allergy is never masked: every clinician must see it, so
  it is the page's status ("Allergy: penicillin", danger, from data) and a side panel. Medicines,
  visits and results are shown as the application passes them.
- **2026-10-16 — Laboratory results are judged by the application (P5.72).** Each result carries
  the laboratory's verdict in words ("In range", "Above range", "Waiting for the laboratory") and
  the application's tone for it; the reference range is data (`low` / `high` decimal strings,
  either may be missing: "3,9–6,1", "< 5,2", "> 1,2", written through `format.number`). Nothing
  compares a value with its range, and a result not yet reported is "—", never 0.
- **2026-10-16 — A trend with its reference range, from the charts as they are (P5.72).** One test
  over time is a small LineChart (200px, 180px on phones) with the result as the main series and
  the laboratory's upper and lower limits as two grey series (`comparison`, `other`) — the range
  as data, so the tooltip and "Show as table" read the limits beside each result; a test with only
  an upper limit draws only that line. No shaded band was added to the charts (it would be a new
  prop whose value the table could not show). The category axis names the month ("Oct 2026",
  `format.monthName`) because a full date is cut at the plot's ends; the tooltip's title
  (`tooltip.label`) and the table under the chart give the day. A test with no reported value
  (vitamin D) shows the chart's own empty state. The test is chosen in the chart's `controls`
  (SelectField) or by pressing a result.
- **2026-10-16 — The chart opens from the clinic (P5.72).** Group H's doctor's calendar and the
  clinic's schedule now open Jovana's chart from her appointment (they opened her finding), the
  clinic's "Patients" tab goes to the chart, the finding's back button returns to the chart, and
  the search (Ctrl K) lists the chart and the lab results. The emergency department appears as her
  visit of 02.08.2026 with dr Jelena Popović (of group G's emergency team); group G's emergency
  department is a staff shift planner with no patients, so there is no patient list there to open
  the chart from.
- **2026-10-16 — Phones: the visits first (P5.72).** On a phone the chart has no key figures above
  the visits: the header, then the visits as cards, then findings, medicines, results and the
  panels under the content (DetailPage's phone layout). The lab results show the chart first, then
  the test's results and the latest results as cards.
